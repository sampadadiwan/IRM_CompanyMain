02 · Private equity

Heavier structures, fewer excuses.

Blockers, feeder vehicles, co-invest entities and multi-tier waterfalls outgrow a spreadsheet quickly. The reporting standard, meanwhile, is set by institutional LPs who audit what you send.

Accounting that holds the structure

  • European and American waterfalls, hurdles, catch-up and carry accrual
  • Multi-entity and multi-currency ledgers closed together
  • Management fee, expense and offset schedules computed, not typed

Oversight of every holding

  • Operating metrics and valuations tracked per portfolio company
  • Variance against plan flagged before the board pack is written
  • Shadow books reconciled continuously alongside your administrator

03 · Family offices & wealth platforms

One consolidated view of everything held.

Capital sits across funds, direct positions and co-investments, reported by different managers in different formats on different dates. Consolidation ends up in a spreadsheet only one person can maintain.

  • Fund commitments and direct positions held in a single record
  • Capital accounts and statements per entity, family branch or mandate
  • Portal access for family members, advisers and auditors, with roles
  • Multi-currency reporting across jurisdictions in one close
  • An always-live assistant that fetches data on demand or generates bespoke statements

04 · Fund administrators & outsourced CFOs

More clients, not more people.

Every client arrives with its own structures, fee terms and reporting calendar. Margin comes from absorbing that without adding a person per fund — and from never sending a number twice.

Run many funds side by side

  • Separate entities, terms and calendars in one workspace
  • Role-based access per client team, with a full audit trail
  • Standard templates applied across funds, overridden where terms differ

Take the repetition out

  • Statements, notices and reports generated from the ledger itself
  • Bank reconciliation with partial, late and over-payment handling
  • AI agents to draft reports and work compliance checklists per client

05 · LPs & institutional investors

Exposure questions answered from the record.

Commitments across managers arrive as PDFs, spreadsheets and quarterly emails. A simple question — how much is drawn, where is it invested, what came back — takes days of re-keying to answer.

  • Commitments, drawdowns and distributions in one register per manager
  • Look-through to underlying holdings where managers share data
  • Notices, capital accounts and tax documents in a single portal
  • Performance and exposure reported without rebuilding a workbook

06 · SPV / syndicate leads

One deal, many small cheques.

The economics of a syndicate live or die on admin per participant. Chasing forty signatures by email, then maintaining forty capital accounts by hand, is the cost that eats the carry.

Raise the vehicle

  • Digital onboarding and e-sign for every participant, however small
  • Commitments, calls and closing tracked per SPV
  • Deal documents, diligence and approvals kept with the vehicle

Close and report

  • Cap table and distributions computed per participant
  • Multi-party transfers and secondaries coordinated on-platform
  • Statements and notices issued without a mail merge
