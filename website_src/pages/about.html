About Us

We build the rail that private capital runs on.

Why we exist

Private markets grew up. Their operations didn't.

Private capital became a multi-trillion asset class. The workflow underneath it did not change much: commitments live in a spreadsheet, KYC packs travel by email, the waterfall is rebuilt by hand each quarter, and the investor statement is retyped from a workbook that only one person fully understands.

Public markets solved this decades ago. Exchanges, depositories, clearing houses and market data systems became shared infrastructure, and everything built on top of them got faster and safer as a result. Private markets never got that layer. Every fund, every administrator and every family office rebuilt a private version of it, badly, on its own.

CapHive is that missing layer. One ledger that holds investors, capital events, portfolio positions and transactions together — so a signature, a wire and a statement are the same event seen from different angles, and the numbers are current on a Tuesday, not eleven weeks after the close.

What we believe

Four convictions the product is built on.

These are not slogans. Each one decided something concrete about how CapHive is designed.

One system of record beats six point solutions

A CRM for LPs, a data room for documents, a workbook for the waterfall and an email thread for approvals will always disagree with each other. Reconciliation between tools is not work anyone should be paid to do. Hold the data once.

Operational alpha is real

Faster onboarding, cleaner books and reporting that arrives when LPs ask for it change the economics of a fund, not just the mood of the ops team. It is the argument in our note “Operational alpha: the game changer AIFs can't ignore”.

Read our insights

AI belongs inside the ledger

An assistant that reads exported PDFs can summarise. An agent that sits on the live record can onboard an investor, analyse a portfolio or run a compliance checklist — with permissions applied and every action logged.

A fund's data belongs to the fund

You should be able to export the full history — investors, capital events, positions, documents — in a usable format, whenever you want, without asking us first. Lock-in by inconvenience is not a business model we're interested in.

How we work with funds

We do the migration. You check the numbers.

Moving a back office is only safe if it is proved rather than promised. Four things we hold ourselves to on every implementation.

We migrate your history

Commitments, capital events, investor records and documents come across as they are — spreadsheets, PDFs and all. That work is ours, not yours.

We run in parallel

One close computed both ways, side by side, until the numbers agree to the rupee or cent. Nothing goes live on a difference nobody can explain.

We stay on your channel

Your team talks to the people who build the product, on the channel they already use. No ticket queue between an operator and an answer.

We ship to your calendar

Reporting deadlines, audit dates and fundraising windows set the roadmap order. Changes land before the quarter that needs them, not after.
