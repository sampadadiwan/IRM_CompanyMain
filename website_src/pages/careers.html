What working here is like

Narrow team, wide surface.

CapHive administers real capital — US$ 10bn+ of AUM and thousands of investors. That shapes how we work more than any culture statement would.

You own a surface end to end

Capital calls, the waterfall engine, the investor portal, an agent — someone owns each of these, from the data model to what an operator sees at 9pm before a deadline. Not a slice of a ticket queue.

Domain depth matters

You will learn how a European waterfall differs from an American one, why a drawdown can be partially funded, and what an LP actually reads in a capital account statement. We teach it, and we expect you to want to know it.

Correctness over velocity

The numbers on the screen are someone's capital account. We move quickly on interfaces and carefully on ledgers — with reconciliation, audit trails and parallel runs, because a rounding error here is a client's money.

Direct access to users

You will sit in implementation sessions and close reviews with CFOs, fund accountants and deal teams. Requirements arrive from the person doing the work, not through three layers of summary.

How we hire

Four steps, and no guesswork.

You will know who you are meeting and what each stage is for. We aim to run the whole process in days rather than months — that is our intent, not a promise, and we will tell you where you stand at each step.

Intro conversation

Thirty minutes with someone on the team you would join. What you have built, what you want to own, and what CapHive actually does.

Practical exercise

A short take-home or timed exercise drawn from real work — a fee calculation, a data model, a piece of UI. No abstract puzzles.

Working session

We go through your exercise together and extend it, the way we would review each other's work. You meet two or three people you would work with daily.

References and offer

Two reference calls, then a written offer with the role, scope and reporting line set out plainly. Questions welcome before you sign.

How we support you

The working conditions.

Deliberately non-numeric — the specifics are discussed in the offer conversation, not advertised.

  • Hybrid working out of the Mumbai office, with focus time protected
  • Health insurance for you and your immediate family
  • Hardware and software you choose, replaced when it slows you down
  • A learning budget for domain courses, certifications and conferences
  • Structured onboarding into private markets — fund structures, waterfalls, reporting
  • Direct time with fund CFOs, accountants and deal teams from your first month
  • A written scope for your role, reviewed with you rather than at you
  • Support to publish or speak about what you build here
