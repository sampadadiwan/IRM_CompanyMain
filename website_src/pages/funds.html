The problem

Fund operations still run on spreadsheets and email.

Every quarter the same scramble: chase documents, rebuild the waterfall, reconcile the bank, retype numbers into statements. The cost isn't just time — it's the risk sitting inside untracked files.

Onboarding drags for weeks

KYC packs travel by email, signatures arrive out of order, and nobody can say which of nine checks is actually outstanding.

Capital events are manual

Drawdown notices are mail-merged one fund at a time, then reconciled against the bank statement by hand, line by line.

The books lag the fund

NAV, DPI and TVPI are only true on the day the close finishes — and stale for the eleven weeks that follow.

Capabilities

Three layers, one ledger.

Investors, capital management and fund accounting share the same underlying record — so a signature, a wire and a statement are all the same event seen from different angles.
