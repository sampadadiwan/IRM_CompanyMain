The lifecycle

Follow the capital, not the feature list.

Most platforms are organised around what they do. A fund is organised around where its money is. These are the five places capital sits, and CapHive holds the record at each of them.

app.caphive.com/overview

Committed

Subscription docs signed

An investor is onboarded and a commitment exists. Nothing has moved yet.

Called

Drawdown issued & reconciled

The fund calls capital, each LP's share is computed, and the money is matched as it lands.

Deployed

Position on the register

A deal closes. Ownership, instrument and price become a position you can report on.

Marked

Valuation & NAV

Company data arrives, the position is marked, and NAV moves the same day.

Returned

Exit, secondary, distribution

Proceeds run the waterfall and reach the investor's capital account.

Every one of those five states is the same object seen at a different moment. That is the whole argument for one platform — and the reason a number never has to be retyped between them.

Before you ask

The five questions everyone asks.

Do we have to take the whole platform?

No. Most funds start with Funds or Portfolios and add the rest as they need it. The modules share one record, so adding a second one later is a configuration step rather than a second migration — but nothing forces you to buy the set. See how pricing works.

Does CapHive replace our fund administrator?

Only if you want it to. Plenty of clients run CapHive as shadow books alongside their administrator, reconciled continuously, which usually makes the relationship easier rather than redundant. Others bring the work in-house once the platform is running.

What happens to our history?

It comes with you. Historic commitments, capital events, positions and documents are migrated and then reconciled against your last audited close — that reconciliation is our work, and nothing goes live on a difference nobody can explain.

Who owns the data?

You do. It is exportable on demand, and there is an API if you want it flowing into your own warehouse. Security & Trust sets out the controls around it.

How long until we are live?

Weeks rather than quarters for a single fund, longer for a multi-entity, multi-currency family of vehicles. The honest answer depends on how clean your history is and how complex the waterfall terms are, which is what the discovery step establishes.
