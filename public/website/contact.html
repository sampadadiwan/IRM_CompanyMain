Let's put CapHive on your fund data.

A walkthrough runs about 40 minutes. It is done by someone who has run fund operations, not by a script — so bring the structures, the fee terms and the quarter that always goes wrong.

What happens on the call

No slides. Your own numbers.

We would rather spend the time on your fund than on a product tour. Four things happen, in this order.

  • We ask about your fund structures, fee terms and reporting calendar
  • We show only the modules that match — funds, portfolios, deals & secondaries, or one of them
  • We talk about migration honestly, including what your team will have to do
  • You get a written scope with sequence, effort and open questions
