Five modules, one ledger.

Why one platform

Stitched point solutions leak.

A fund admin tool, a portfolio tracker, a data room and a spreadsheet for the waterfall can each be good software. The gaps between them are where the errors live.

One record, no rekeying

A commitment entered at onboarding is the same commitment the drawdown calls, the capital account carries and the LP statement prints. Nobody retypes it.

Every action audited

Who changed a valuation, who approved a transfer, who sent which notice and when — recorded on the object itself, not reconstructed from an inbox.

Numbers current daily

Computations run as events land, so NAV, TVPI and exposure are true today. The quarterly close becomes a review rather than a rebuild.

AI Agents

Agents that work inside the ledger.

Because the whole lifecycle sits on one record, an agent can answer across it — a fund's DPI after the March distribution, a portfolio company's variance to plan, the open items on a compliance checklist. Same permissions as your team, every action logged.
