The problem

Fund operations still run on spreadsheets and email.

Every quarter the same scramble: chase documents, rebuild the waterfall, reconcile the bank, retype numbers into statements. The cost isn't just time — it's the risk sitting inside untracked files.

Onboarding drags for weeks

KYC packs travel by email, signatures arrive out of order, and nobody can say which of nine checks is actually outstanding.

Capital events are manual

Drawdown notices are mail-merged one fund at a time, then reconciled against the bank statement by hand, line by line.

The books lag the fund

NAV, DPI and TVPI are only true on the day the close finishes — and stale for the eleven weeks that follow.

Capabilities

Three layers, one ledger.

Investors, capital management and fund accounting share the same underlying record — so a signature, a wire and a statement are all the same event seen from different angles.

Investors

Onboarding that finishes in days.

Send one link. The investor completes KYC, uploads documents and e-signs the subscription pack, while your team watches a live checklist rather than an inbox.

  • Digital KYC / AML with document capture and expiry tracking
  • E-signed subscription packs, side letters and capital commitments
  • Investor portal for statements, notices, capital account and tax documents
  • Segmented notifications and engagement history per investor

Capital Management

Drawdowns and distributions in clicks.

Build a capital event once, and CapHive computes each investor's share, generates the notice, sends it, and reconciles the money as it lands.

  • Pro-rata and bespoke drawdown calculations across commitments
  • Notices generated, branded and dispatched to every LP at once
  • Bank reconciliation with partial, late and over-payment handling
  • Distributions, recallable capital and return-of-capital tracking

Fund Accounting

Books that are current every day.

A computation engine keeps NAV, capital accounts, fees and the waterfall live — so the quarterly close becomes a review, not a rebuild.

  • Daily NAV, capital accounts and investor-level statements
  • European and American waterfalls, hurdles, catch-up and carry accrual
  • Management fee, expense and offset schedules computed automatically
  • Shadow books alongside your administrator, reconciled continuously
  • Multi-currency and multi-entity ledgers in a single close
