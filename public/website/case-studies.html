Anatomy of an engagement

How a rollout actually runs.

Every engagement above followed the same shape. Migration is our work, not yours: we take the spreadsheets and documents you already have and reconcile the platform back to your last audited close.

Weeks, not quarters

One plan, tracked in the open.

You get a dated plan on day one, a single channel for questions, and a status view your team and ours read from the same place.

  • Fund structures, fee terms and waterfall mechanics documented before any data moves
  • Historic commitments, capital events and investor records loaded and reconciled
  • One close computed both ways until the numbers agree to the rupee or cent
  • Sign-off recorded against each fund before it goes live
