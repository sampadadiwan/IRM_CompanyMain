SIMPLE AGREEMENT FOR FUTURE EQUITY (SAFE)
NovaTech Ventures Inc. — Seed Round

This SAFE Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of April 18, 2026, by and between
NovaTech Ventures Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), and the undersigned investor.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. INVESTMENT TERMS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Investment Amount:         USD 3,500,000 (Three Million Five Hundred Thousand US Dollars)
Instrument Type:           Equity Shares (Series Seed Preferred)
Pre-Money Valuation:       USD 12,000,000 (Twelve Million US Dollars)
Post-Money Valuation:      USD 15,500,000 (Fifteen Million Five Hundred Thousand US Dollars)
Equity Percentage:         22.6% (fully diluted, post-round)
Closing Date:              May 15, 2026

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. GOVERNANCE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Board Seats:
  - Lead Investor shall have the right to appoint one (1) Board Director.
  - The Company's Founders shall retain two (2) Board seats.
  - One (1) independent Director to be mutually agreed within 90 days of closing.
  - Total Board composition post-closing: 4 Directors.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. INVESTOR RIGHTS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Anti-Dilution:
  The Series Seed Preferred Shares shall carry broad-based weighted-average anti-dilution
  protection. In the event of a down-round financing, the conversion price shall be adjusted
  using the weighted-average formula as defined in Schedule A.

Pro-Rata Rights:
  Each investor holding more than 5% of outstanding shares on a fully diluted basis shall have
  the right to participate in any future equity financing on a pro-rata basis up to their then-
  current percentage ownership. This right shall be exercisable within 20 business days of the
  Company's written notice of a new round.

Right of First Refusal (ROFR):
  The Company and Investors (in that order of priority) shall have a right of first refusal on
  any proposed transfer of shares by existing shareholders. The ROFR shall be exercisable within
  30 days of receipt of a transfer notice specifying the proposed transferee and price.

Drag-Along Rights:
  If holders of 65% or more of outstanding Series Seed Preferred Shares approve a proposed
  Acquisition Event, all other holders of Series Seed Preferred shall be obligated to consent
  to and raise no objection to such transaction (drag-along obligation).

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. LOCK-UP AND TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Lock-Up Period:
  All shares issued under this Agreement shall be subject to a lock-up period of twelve (12)
  months from the Closing Date. During the lock-up period, no shareholder may sell, transfer,
  pledge, or otherwise dispose of any shares without prior written consent of the Board.
