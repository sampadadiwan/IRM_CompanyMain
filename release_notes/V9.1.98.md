# Release Notes - V9.1.98

## Overview
This release introduces significant enhancements to our AI-powered portfolio reporting and improves the transparency of capital call notices with detailed folio-level fee breakdowns.

## What's New

### 🤖 Enhanced AI Portfolio Assistant
We've upgraded the AI Assistant within Portfolio Reports to provide a more integrated and capable experience.
- **Improved Document Analysis**: The AI now has better access to company pitch decks, financials, and product overviews linked to your reports, allowing for more accurate data extraction and insights.
- **Web & News Search**: The assistant can now search the live web for the latest company news and market developments when information isn't available in your uploaded documents.
- **Unified Chat Interface**: We've transitioned to a new, standardized chat panel that stays open while you work on different sections of your report.
- **Markdown & Link Support**: Chat responses now support rich formatting and clickable links for easier navigation to sources.

### 📄 Capital Call Notice Enhancements
To provide better transparency to investors, we've updated the Capital Call document generation:
- **Folio-Level Fee Breakdown**: Call notices can now include a detailed breakdown of capital fees, other fees, and investment amounts at the folio level.
- **Aggregated Context**: The system now calculates total amounts across all folios within a single call, providing a clearer picture of the overall transaction alongside individual folio details.

### 📁 Document Management
- **Evaluation Documents**: Added new capabilities to upload and view evaluation-specific documents within the Portfolio Report folder structure.

## Technical & Internal Changes
- **Refactored AI Architecture**: Replaced the legacy `PortfolioChatAgent` and `AiChatMessages` models with a more robust `PortfolioChatProvider` and centralized `SupportAgent` framework.
- **New Document Extraction Service**: Introduced `FolderDocumentExtractor` for more reliable text extraction from PDF, Excel, and PowerPoint files for AI processing.
- **Route Optimization**: Cleaned up AI-related routes and consolidated controllers for better maintainability.
