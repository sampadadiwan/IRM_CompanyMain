# Fund Unit Allocation System: Technical & Business Overview

This document provides a walkthrough of the technical details and business outcomes generated by the unit allocation logic in the IRM system.

## 1. Overview
The Fund Unit system tracks investor ownership within a fund. When capital is called (inflow) or distributed (outflow), the system calculates the corresponding number of "units" issued or redeemed to maintain an accurate cap table for the fund.

## 2. Technical Components

### FundUnitsJob
Located at: [`app/packs/funds/funds/jobs/fund_units_job.rb`](app/packs/funds/funds/jobs/fund_units_job.rb)

- **Purpose**: Asynchronous entry point for unit calculations.
- **Idempotency**: Designed to be callable multiple times for the same transaction without duplicating units (logic handled in the engine).
- **Execution Strategy**: Uses `Chewy.strategy(:sidekiq)` to ensure that search indexes for fund units are updated efficiently after processing.
- **Routing**: Dispatches work to the `DefaultUnitAllocationEngine` based on whether the owner is a `CapitalCall` or a `CapitalDistribution`.

### DefaultUnitAllocationEngine
Located at: [`app/packs/funds/funds/services/default_unit_allocation_engine.rb`](app/packs/funds/funds/services/default_unit_allocation_engine.rb)

This service implements the core mathematical and business logic:

#### A. Issuance (Capital Calls)
- **Validation**: Only processes `verified` remittances with positive collected amounts.
- **Unit Pricing**: Calculates total cost per unit by adding `price` and `premium` from the Capital Call configuration.
- **Quantity Formula**: `Quantity = Amount / (Price + Premium)`.
- **Dating Logic**: Sets the `issue_date` to the later of the `payment_date` or the `due_date`, ensuring alignment with fund accounting rules.

#### B. Redemption (Distributions)
- **Validation**: Only processes `completed` distribution payments.
- **Quantity Formula**: `Quantity = - (Amount / Price)`.
- **Ownership Reduction**: Records are created with negative quantities to reflect the reduction in investor holdings.

#### C. Error Handling & Monitoring
- **Skip Logic**: Automatically skips generation if units are already allocated, or if critical data (like unit prices or types) is missing.
- **Notifications**: Broadcasts real-time success/failure alerts to the triggering user via `UserAlert` and sends detailed error reports via `EntityMailer` if specific records fail to process.

## 3. Business Outcomes

- **Automated Cap Table Accuracy**: Eliminates manual spreadsheets for unitization, ensuring investor ownership percentages are always up-to-date and accurate.
- **Auditability & Transparency**: Every unit record is explicitly linked to the underlying cash transaction (remittance or payment), providing a verifiable trail for auditors.
- **Efficiency at Scale**: Allows fund managers to process hundreds of allocations simultaneously across different unit types and premium structures with a single click.
- **Financial Integrity**: Enforces strict validation rules (e.g., verification status, positive amounts) to prevent accounting errors in fund unit issuance.
- **Investor Trust**: Provides timely and accurate reporting of unit holdings, which is the primary metric of value for fund investors.
