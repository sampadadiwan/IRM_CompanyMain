KPI YTD, Quarter and Year from Mixed Monthly and Quarterly Data — Proposal

Audience: Business team, product, QA, support — non-technical. New to this? Start with the Workflow Journey — every action, in order, from start to the outcome.

Planning phase — no code changes. This document proposes and explains the change. Do not edit application code while writing it; implementation happens later, in the tasks phase.

Why

A portfolio company uploaded its Q1 as one quarterly figure and then July and August as monthly figures. The platform ignored the Q1 figure when building "year to date" (YTD), so July YTD showed July alone and August YTD showed July + August. It also produced a September quarter figure from only two months, treating the missing month as zero. Fund managers read these numbers as real, so they are wrong and misleading.

Two things read these figures downstream and are wrong or missing today, so they are fixed in the same change:

  • The change shown next to a YTD figure compares it with the previous month's YTD (June YTD against May YTD), not with the same point one year earlier. A YTD year-over-year change is meaningless when measured that way.
  • The Word portfolio report has no way to show Actual against Budget (for a month, quarter, YTD or year), so authors compute it by hand.

What Changes

  • YTD uses whatever granularity exists. YTD at any month end is everything from the start of the fiscal year up to that date, built from quarter figures where a quarter was uploaded as a quarter and from monthly figures otherwise. June 2026 YTD equals Q1 2026 (India fiscal year, April to March).
  • A gap stops YTD. If a month is missing and no quarter figure covers it, no YTD is shown from that month to the end of that fiscal year. A missing month is never treated as zero. A later quarter figure that covers the gap lets YTD resume.
  • Quarter and Year are only calculated from complete data. A calculated Quarter needs all 3 months. A calculated Year needs all 4 quarters covered. With July and August but no September, no September quarter is produced.
  • Uploaded quarter figures are never overwritten. A quarter that someone uploaded stays as uploaded. Only figures the platform calculated are recalculated.
  • Stale calculated figures are removed. If a calculated Quarter/YTD/Year no longer qualifies (for example the September quarter above, or after a month is un-approved), the platform removes it. Figures a person entered are never removed.
  • Forward-fill KPIs (balances such as cash) carry the latest balance instead of adding. A balance is a snapshot, so adding months together would be meaningless (cash of 10 in June and 12 in July is not 22). Its YTD at a date is the balance at that date, which equals that month's own value; this is how forward fill already works. A KPI mapped to "Cumulate" (revenue) is different: its YTD is the running total and differs from the month. A balance's YTD does not need the earlier months to exist. A Quarter is shown only when all 3 months exist, and a Year only when all 4 quarters are covered.
  • One definition of the fiscal year is used everywhere: the portfolio company's own setting, then the fund's setting, then the regional default (April for India, January for US). Until now the calculation ignored the company and fund settings and always used the regional default, so a company with a different fiscal year start will see its YTD, Quarter and Year figures rebuilt on its own fiscal year.
  • One calculation at a time per company. If two approvals for the same company arrive together, the second waits for the first, so the final figures always reflect everything approved.
  • An uploaded figure replaces a calculated one for good. If a person uploads and approves a Quarter, YTD or Year figure where the system had calculated one, it is treated as uploaded from then on and is never recalculated or removed.
  • YTD change is year over year. The percentage change on a YTD figure compares it with the YTD figure of the same series dated exactly one year earlier (30 Jun 2026 against 30 Jun 2025). If there is no such figure, or it is zero, the change is empty ("N/A"); the system never falls back to an earlier month. Month, Quarter and Year figures keep comparing with the previous figure of their series (month on month, quarter on quarter). Existing data is corrected by the existing percentage-change recompute, which "Recompute Cumulative" now runs as well. A change too large to store (above 9,999.99%) is left empty. An empty change shows "-" in the grid and never breaks a colour threshold.
  • Actual against Budget in the portfolio report. For every Actual figure the report also offers "Actual as a percentage of Budget" for the same date and period (Actual ÷ Budget × 100, so 92 means 92%). On a YTD column this is YTD Actual against YTD Budget; on a Quarter column it is Quarter Actual against Quarter Budget. It is empty ("N/A") when there is no approved Budget figure or Budget is zero. Only approved figures count, on both sides; an unapproved Actual or Budget is treated as missing. It is calculated when the report is generated and is not stored, so approving a Budget later never leaves it stale. Budget is the only comparator now; the comparator list is built so another tag (for example IC) can be added without reshaping the report.
  • The Word report uses approved KPIs only. The report's KPI rows (the main list and the per-category lists) take only approved figures; an unapproved figure shows "N/A" like a missing one. The same rule applies to the Word export of an AI-drafted report, which mirrors the generator. The KPI screens still show unapproved figures, because reviewers need to see them there.
  • BREAKING (behaviour): a Quarter figure built from only 1 or 2 months is no longer produced. Existing scenarios that relied on partial-quarter totals change.
  • BREAKING (behaviour): the percentage change on a YTD figure changes meaning (previous month becomes the same date one year earlier). Values already shown in the grid and used for the red/green thresholds change after the recompute.

What goes wrong

Aug 2026 is missing but Sep 2026 is uploaded. Jul YTD is shown (Q1 + Jul). Aug YTD and Sep YTD are not shown, and the Sep quarter is not calculated. The fix is to upload Aug, or to upload the Q2 total as a quarter figure; after approval the missing figures appear.

User Stories

US-1: YTD includes an uploaded quarter

As a fund manager, I want YTD to include a quarter that was uploaded as a quarter, So that July YTD is Q1 plus July.

Acceptance criteria:

  • With Q1 (Apr to Jun) uploaded and July uploaded, Jun YTD equals Q1 and Jul YTD equals Q1 plus July.
  • Aug YTD equals Q1 plus July plus August.

US-2: No guessing for missing months

As a fund manager, I want missing periods never to be treated as zero, So that no quarter, YTD or year figure understates the truth.

Acceptance criteria:

  • July and August without September produces no September quarter.
  • A missing month stops YTD for the rest of that fiscal year unless a quarter figure covers the gap.

US-3: Uploaded figures win

As a fund manager, I want figures I or the company uploaded never to be replaced by calculated ones, So that the reported number is the one on record.

Acceptance criteria:

  • An uploaded Quarter figure keeps its value even when all 3 months exist.
  • Only calculated figures are recalculated or removed.

US-4: Wrong figures clean themselves up

As a fund manager, I want wrongly calculated figures to be removed when they no longer qualify, So that the grid does not keep showing the earlier mistake.

Acceptance criteria:

  • A calculated Quarter, YTD or Year that no longer qualifies disappears after the next approval or recompute.

US-5: Balances show the latest value

As a fund manager, I want balance-type KPIs (forward fill) to show the latest balance, So that a quarter-end balance is only shown when the quarter is complete, and a missing month does not hide the others.

Acceptance criteria:

  • A Quarter balance is shown only when all 3 months exist, and equals the last month's value.
  • YTD for a balance equals the balance at that date, even when an earlier month is missing.

US-6: YTD change is year over year

As a fund manager, I want the change shown next to a YTD figure to compare it with the same date a year earlier, So that I read real growth and not just the month's addition to the running total.

Acceptance criteria:

  • Jun-26 YTD is compared with Jun-25 YTD of the same KPI and tag.
  • With no figure a year earlier, or a year-earlier figure of zero, the change is empty; it is never taken against an earlier month.
  • Month figures still compare with the previous month and Quarter figures with the previous quarter.
  • Existing YTD changes are corrected by the percentage-change recompute.

US-7: Actual against Budget in the report

As a fund manager, I want the portfolio report to give Actual as a percentage of Budget for the same date and period, So that I do not divide by hand.

Acceptance criteria:

  • For each Actual column the report offers Actual ÷ Budget × 100 against the Budget column of the same period and date (YTD against YTD, Quarter against Quarter).
  • It is "N/A" when the Budget figure is missing, unapproved or zero, and when the Actual figure is unapproved.
  • It always reflects the Budget approved at the time the report is generated.
  • The report's KPI rows use approved figures only: an unapproved figure shows "N/A" in <label> and <label>_percent as well.
  • Otherwise existing report fields are unchanged for approved figures.

Impact

  • The KPI cumulation logic for cumulate and forward-fill KPIs (portfolio company KPI pack).
  • The KPI grid: Quarter, YTD and Year cells change value or become N/A.
  • Existing cumulation scenarios that assumed partial-quarter totals.
  • Data: wrongly calculated rows in existing companies are removed on the next cumulation or recompute.
  • Companies whose fiscal year start is set on the company or in the fund settings (and differs from the regional default) get their YTD and Year figures rebuilt on that fiscal year; the first run is the test investor.
  • The stored percentage change of YTD figures changes meaning; existing values are corrected by the existing percentage-change recompute (no schema change).
  • Other places that show the stored percentage change (KPI show page and export, tear sheet data, AI portfolio assistant data, and the change figure beside each KPI in the Word portfolio report) show the new YTD meaning too; none of their code changes. A YTD figure with no year-earlier figure has an empty change there.
  • The Word portfolio report (and the Word export of an AI-drafted report) stops showing unapproved KPI figures; they show "N/A".
  • The Word portfolio report generator gains one field per Actual KPI column. Its KPI-row building also stops running a query per KPI mapping per report.

Benefits

  1. YTD matches what the company actually reported, whether quarterly, monthly or mixed.
  2. No more silent zeros: a number is shown only when the data behind it is complete.
  3. Uploaded numbers are never replaced by calculated ones.
  4. Earlier wrong figures correct themselves.
  5. The change next to a YTD figure shows real year-over-year growth.
  6. Actual against Budget is in the report, always current, with no hand calculation.

FAQ

Question Answer
Who sees this? Anyone who reads the KPI grid; no new screens.
What happens to figures that are already wrong? Calculated ones are removed on the next approval or recompute, and rebuilt if they qualify.
Will uploaded figures change? No. Uploaded Quarter, YTD and Year figures are never changed or removed.
Why is September YTD missing when I uploaded September? A month before it is missing and nothing covers it. Upload the missing month or the quarter.
Does this change notifications or billing? No.
Does this affect Budget or IC figures? Yes, the same completeness rules apply to each tag, and each tag's YTD change is measured against its own tag a year earlier.
Why is the change next to my YTD figure now "-"? There is no YTD figure of that KPI and tag dated one year earlier, or it is zero. It is not compared with an earlier month.
Why did the YTD change values move? They used to compare with last month's YTD; they now compare with the same date last year.
Is "Actual against Budget" a gap or a ratio? A ratio: Actual ÷ Budget × 100. 92 means Actual is 92% of Budget.
What if I approve Budget after generating a report? Generate the report again; nothing is stored.
Will IC work the same way? It is planned as an additional comparator with its own field, added when the template needs it.
Where is the how? In the engineering design document.

Design Principle for the Next Phase

Keep the change inside the existing cumulation code: one pass that settles quarters and one pass that walks the fiscal year, replacing the separate monthly and quarterly paths and their "skip years" workaround. Reuse the existing figure writer, fiscal-year helper and approval trigger. The one risky area is telling calculated figures from uploaded ones reliably, because both removal and "never overwrite" depend on it.

The two additions stay small. The YTD year-over-year fix is one comparator rule inside the existing percentage-change recompute, which already runs after cumulation. Actual against Budget is plain arithmetic on figures the report generator already loads, added to its existing row building (indexed once in memory). No new column, job, or template parsing.
