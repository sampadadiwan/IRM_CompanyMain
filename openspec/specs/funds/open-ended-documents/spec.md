# funds/open-ended-documents Specification

## Purpose

Sends evergreen investors the documents and emails an evergreen fund normally issues, in place of call and distribution notices: acknowledgements, payment instructions, contract notes, gate notices, redemption advices and cancellations. Contract notes and redemption advices are generated as PDFs only when the fund has a template for them, as a step of the dealing date (generate, then approve and send); without a template they go out as emails straight away.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Evergreen emails replace call and distribution notices
For evergreen funds the system SHALL send the emails below to the investor's users who receive the fund's notices today, and SHALL NOT send capital call or distribution notices. Dates in these emails SHALL use CapHive's email date format.

| Email | Sent when |
|---|---|
| Subscription acknowledgement | A subscription is accepted (with payment instructions if pre-funded) |
| Payment instructions | A pay-after-dealing dealing date is confirmed |
| Contract note | Units are allotted, when the fund has no contract-note template; otherwise when the dealing date's documents are approved and sent (with the PDF attached) |
| Redemption acknowledgement | A redemption is accepted |
| Gate notice | A redemption is partially filled or not filled |
| Redemption advice | A redemption payment is marked paid and its units cancelled, when the fund has no redemption-advice template; otherwise when the dealing date's documents are approved and sent (with the PDF attached) |
| Request cancelled | An order is cancelled, or rolled or cancelled at cut-off |

Each contract note and redemption advice SHALL be emailed once per fill.

#### Scenario: Acknowledgement with instructions
- **WHEN** a pre-funded subscription of ₹2,50,00,000 for 1 Dec 2026 is accepted
- **THEN** the investor receives "Subscription received" stating the amount, class, dealing date 1 Dec 2026, "Pay by Nov 26, 2026" and the bank details and reference

#### Scenario: No call notice
- **WHEN** a dealing date of an evergreen fund is confirmed
- **THEN** no capital call notice or distribution notice is sent to any investor

#### Scenario: Gate notice
- **WHEN** B LLP's ₹2,50,00,000 redemption is filled ₹2,11,00,000 with ₹39,00,000 (381.6047 units) carried to 1 Dec 2026
- **THEN** B LLP receives a gate notice with the filled amount, the carried units and the next dealing date

### Requirement: Contract note
Every allotment SHALL produce a contract note showing folio, class, NAV date, dealing date, NAV per unit, units allotted and amount.
- **No contract-note template:** the system SHALL email the contract note as soon as the units are allotted, with these figures in the email body and no PDF.
- **Template present:** the system SHALL NOT email anything at allotment. The contract note SHALL be generated by *Generate documents* and emailed, with the PDF attached, by *Approve and send*.

#### Scenario: Contract note contents
- **WHEN** 4,892.3679 class A units are allotted to folio F-101 on 2 Nov 2026 at ₹10,220.00 (NAV 31 Oct 2026)
- **THEN** the contract note shows F-101, Class A, NAV date Oct 31, 2026, dealing date Nov 02, 2026, ₹10,220.00, 4,892.3679 units and ₹5,00,00,000

#### Scenario: No template — email at allotment
- **WHEN** Alpha has no "Contract Note Template" and P Family Trust's 4,892.3679 units are allotted at confirmation on 2 Nov 2026
- **THEN** P Family Trust's users receive the contract note email straight away with the figures in its body and no attachment, and no document is stored

### Requirement: Redemption advice
Every paid redemption SHALL produce a redemption advice showing units cancelled, NAV per unit, gross amount, early-redemption fee, net paid and payment date.
- **No redemption-advice template:** the system SHALL email the advice as soon as the payment is marked paid and the units cancelled, with these figures in the email body and no PDF.
- **Template present:** the system SHALL NOT email anything when the redemption is paid. The advice SHALL be generated by *Generate documents* and emailed, with the PDF attached, by *Approve and send*.

#### Scenario: Advice with fee
- **WHEN** a 1,200-unit redemption at ₹10,220.00 with a 2 % early-redemption fee is paid on 10 Nov 2026
- **THEN** the advice shows 1,200 units, gross ₹1,22,64,000, fee ₹2,45,280, net ₹1,20,18,720 and payment date Nov 10, 2026

#### Scenario: No email at payment when a template exists
- **WHEN** Alpha has a "Redemption Advice Template" and Asha marks A Ltd's redemption paid on 10 Nov 2026
- **THEN** the units are cancelled, no advice email is sent, and the tracker row shows the advice as waiting to be generated

### Requirement: Document templates are optional
A fund MAY have a template of each kind, uploaded to the fund's documents with the tag "Contract Note Template" or "Redemption Advice Template". A folio MAY override the fund's template with its own template of the same name. The two kinds SHALL be independent: a kind without a template SHALL follow the email-only path while the other kind follows the template path. The system SHALL NOT generate a document from a built-in layout.

#### Scenario: Only one kind has a template
- **WHEN** Alpha has a "Contract Note Template" but no "Redemption Advice Template", and on 2 Nov 2026 P Family Trust is allotted units and A Ltd's redemption is marked paid
- **THEN** A Ltd's redemption advice email is sent straight away without a PDF, while P Family Trust's contract note waits for Generate documents

#### Scenario: Folio template overrides fund template
- **WHEN** folio F-101 has its own "Contract Note Template" with the same name as the fund's, and Generate documents runs for 2 Nov 2026
- **THEN** P Family Trust's contract note is generated from F-101's template and every other contract note from the fund's

### Requirement: Generate documents for a dealing date
A confirmed dealing date SHALL offer *Generate documents* in its Actions menu when the fund has a template of either kind. It SHALL run in the background, with the usual document-generation progress, and generate:
- a contract note for every allotted subscription fill whose kind has a template and which has no document yet;
- a redemption advice for every paid redemption fill whose kind has a template and which has no document yet.

Each document SHALL be generated from the template (Word template filled with the fill's figures, saved as PDF) and saved unapproved on the folio. Investors SHALL NOT see unapproved generated documents. Running it again SHALL generate only for fills settled since the last run. When a document cannot be generated, the others SHALL still be generated and the user SHALL be told which failed.

#### Scenario: Generate documents from templates
- **WHEN** Alpha has both templates, the 2 Nov 2026 dealing date has P Family Trust allotted and A Ltd and B LLP paid, and Asha clicks Generate documents
- **THEN** one contract note (F-101) and two redemption advices (F-012, F-031) are saved unapproved on the folios, no email is sent, and the tracker rows show "Generated — awaiting approval"

#### Scenario: Re-run picks up only new settlements
- **WHEN** the 1 Dec 2026 documents were generated, approved and sent for P Family Trust, Q Capital's money is then recorded on 6 Dec and its units allotted, and Asha clicks Generate documents again
- **THEN** only Q Capital's contract note is generated, and P Family Trust's is not regenerated or re-sent

#### Scenario: Nothing to generate
- **WHEN** every settled fill of the dealing date already has its document and Asha clicks Generate documents
- **THEN** no job runs and she sees "Nothing to generate — every settled order already has its document or email"

#### Scenario: One document fails
- **WHEN** B LLP's redemption advice cannot be generated from its template during a run that also generates P Family Trust's and A Ltd's
- **THEN** P Family Trust's and A Ltd's documents are saved, Asha is told B LLP's failed, and B LLP's row shows "Not generated — Regenerate"

#### Scenario: Unapproved documents hidden from investors
- **WHEN** P Family Trust opens Documents after its contract note is generated but before it is approved
- **THEN** the contract note is not listed; after Approve and send it is listed

### Requirement: Approve and send documents
A dealing date with generated, unapproved documents SHALL offer *Approve and send*, available only to users allowed to approve documents (the approver role). It SHALL first show a confirmation summarising what will happen ("Approve 1 contract note and 2 redemption advices, and email them to investors"). On confirmation it SHALL approve every generated document of the dealing date and email each investor their contract note or redemption advice with the PDF attached, using the fund's custom evergreen wording when one exists. A document already sent SHALL NOT be sent again.

#### Scenario: Approve and send
- **WHEN** Ravi clicks Approve and send on 2 Nov 2026 after one contract note and two redemption advices were generated, and confirms the popup
- **THEN** the three documents are approved, P Family Trust receives the contract note email with the PDF attached, A Ltd and B LLP receive their redemption advices with PDFs, and each row shows the document sent

#### Scenario: Approve requires approver
- **WHEN** a fund admin without the approver role opens the dealing date, or posts Approve and send directly
- **THEN** the Approve and send button is not shown and the request is refused

### Requirement: View and regenerate a document
Each tracker row with a generated document SHALL offer *View*, and *Regenerate* while the document is unapproved. Regenerate SHALL replace the unapproved document with a new one from the current template. An approved document SHALL NOT be regenerated.

#### Scenario: Regenerate while unapproved
- **WHEN** Asha corrects the contract-note template and clicks Regenerate on P Family Trust's unapproved contract note
- **THEN** the old document is replaced by a new unapproved one generated from the corrected template

#### Scenario: Regenerate after approval
- **WHEN** P Family Trust's contract note is approved and Asha posts Regenerate for it directly
- **THEN** the row offers no Regenerate action, the request is refused with "Approved documents can't be regenerated", and the document is unchanged
