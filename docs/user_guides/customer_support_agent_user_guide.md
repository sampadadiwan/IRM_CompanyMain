# Customer Support Agent — User Guide

The Customer Support Agent is an AI-powered chatbot built into the CapHive platform. It helps Fund Manager (FM) users get instant answers to platform support questions — how to use features, troubleshoot issues, and understand their data — without waiting for the support team.

> **Where to find it:** Sidebar → [**Dashboard**](/entities/dashboard), then the **"Need help? Ask Customer Support Agent"** bubble in the bottom-right corner (fund entities, once CapHive has enabled the agent with chat on). CapHive support staff review feedback and escalations from [**CS Reviews**](/cs_feedback_reviews) in the sidebar, which needs the support role.

When the agent cannot answer a question, it escalates the conversation to the CapHive support team, who review and respond through the CS Feedback Review Dashboard.

This guide is divided into two parts:

1. **For Fund Manager Users** — How to use the support chat to get help
2. **For the CapHive Support Team** — How to use the CS Feedback Review Dashboard to monitor quality, handle escalations, and take action

# Part 1: For Fund Manager Users

## Getting Started

The Customer Support Agent is automatically available on your CapHive dashboard if your entity (organisation) has the feature enabled. There is nothing to install or configure — simply look for the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of your dashboard.

### Who Can Use It

All FM users within an enabled entity can access the support chat:
- Company Admins
- Operations Managers
- Operations Users
- Any employee-level user

### What You Need

- A web browser logged into CapHive
- An active CapHive account within an entity that has the Customer Support Agent enabled

---

## Opening the Chat

When you navigate to your FM dashboard, you will see a floating chat bubble in the bottom-right corner of the screen:

- A speech-tail label reads **"Need help? Ask Customer Support Agent"**
- Next to it is a circular blue button with a robot icon

**To open the chat**, click anywhere on the bubble or the blue button. A chat panel will slide up from the bottom-right corner.

**To close the chat**, click the **X** (Close) button in the chat panel header. The bubble is hidden while the panel is open and reappears when you close it.

The chat panel can also be:
- **Maximised** — Click the arrows icon (Maximize) to expand the panel to full screen. Click again to restore.
- **Dragged** — Click and hold the blue header bar to drag the panel to a different position on screen.
- **Resized** — Drag the edges of the panel to resize it.

---

## Asking Questions

Once the chat is open, you will see a welcome screen with a text input at the bottom.

### Quick-Start Suggestions

The welcome screen shows sample questions you can try:

- "How do I create a capital call?"
- "What's the process for investor onboarding?"
- "How do I generate a distribution notice?"

Click any suggestion to auto-fill it in the input box, then press **Enter** or click the **Send** button.

### Typing Your Own Question

1. Click the text input at the bottom of the chat panel
2. Type your question in plain English
3. Press **Enter** or click the blue Send button

The agent will show a "Thinking..." spinner while it processes your question. Responses typically appear within a few seconds.

---

## What the Agent Can Help With

The Customer Support Agent draws on three sources of information to answer your questions:

### 1. Platform User Guides
The agent has access to all CapHive user guides covering features such as:
- Capital calls, distributions, and commitments
- Investor onboarding and KYC
- Fund management and reporting
- Document management
- Dashboard widgets and customisation
- Notification preferences
- And many more

**Best for:** "How do I..." questions, step-by-step instructions, feature explanations.

### 2. Your Entity's Live Data
The agent can query your organisation's actual data in CapHive — investors, funds, capital calls, KYC records, and more. All queries are scoped to your entity only; the agent cannot see other organisations' data.

**Best for:** "Show me..." or "How many..." questions, data lookups, status checks.

### 3. Your Past Conversations
The agent can search your previous support conversations for context on recurring questions or follow-up queries.

**Best for:** Continuing a previous conversation topic, referencing something you asked before.

### What It Cannot Help With

- **General knowledge** unrelated to CapHive (e.g. investment advice, market data)
- **Actions that change data** — the agent is read-only; it cannot create, update, or delete records on your behalf
- **Other organisations' data** — the agent is strictly scoped to your entity
- **Highly technical or engineering-level questions** — for these, the agent will escalate to the support team

---

## Understanding Responses

The agent formats responses using rich text:

- **Headers** for organising sections
- **Bold text** for emphasis on important terms and menu paths
- **Numbered steps** for step-by-step instructions
- **Bullet points** for lists
- **Tables** for structured data
- **Code blocks** for technical references

When the agent references a specific CapHive feature, it will typically include the navigation path (e.g. "Go to **Stakeholders → View Stakeholders**, open the stakeholder, then the **Users** tab") so you can follow along in the platform.

---

## Rating Responses

After every agent response, you will see two icons beneath the message:

- **Thumbs Up** — Click if the response was helpful and accurate
- **Thumbs Down** — Click if the response was unhelpful, incorrect, or incomplete

### Why Rating Matters

Your ratings directly help improve the agent:
- **Positive ratings** confirm that the agent is answering correctly
- **Negative ratings** flag responses for review by the CapHive support team, who use them to improve guides and agent behaviour

Rating is optional but encouraged — it takes one click and makes a real difference.

---

## Providing Feedback Notes

When you click **Thumbs Down**, a text input appears below the message:

1. Type a brief note explaining what was wrong (e.g. "The steps are outdated" or "It didn't mention the new KYC tab")
2. Click **Send** to submit your note

Your feedback note is attached to the response and visible to the support team when they review flagged items. The more specific your note, the faster the team can improve the answer.

## When the Agent Escalates to a Human

Sometimes the agent determines it cannot fully answer your question. When this happens:

1. The agent tells you, in its own words, that it is handing your question to the CapHive support team
2. The chat session is marked as **escalated**
3. An email notification is sent to the CapHive support team with your question and conversation context
4. The escalation appears in the support team's review dashboard

### What Happens Next

- **You can continue chatting** in the same session — the agent will still answer your new questions.
- Each subsequent response will include a note at the top: *"Note: Your earlier query has been escalated to our support team and is being reviewed."* This reminds you that the escalated issue is still being handled by the support team.
- The support team will review your escalated question and may contact you directly. The platform does not email you automatically.
- Once the support team resolves the escalation, the note will no longer appear.

You do **not** need to start a new conversation after an escalation — the agent remains available to help with other questions while the escalated issue is being reviewed separately.

### Reasons for Escalation

The agent may escalate for several reasons:
- **You requested it** — You asked to speak to a human
- **Low confidence** — The agent is not confident in its answer
- **Complex issue** — The question requires human judgment
- **Maximum attempts reached** — The agent tried multiple approaches but could not find a satisfactory answer

## Managing Chat Sessions

### Starting a New Conversation

Click the **New** button in the chat panel header to start a fresh conversation. This creates a new session — your previous conversation is saved and accessible via History.

### Viewing Past Sessions

Click the **History** button in the chat panel header. A sidebar slides open showing your past sessions with the most recent at the top. Click any session to load its messages.

### Session Auto-Expire

If you have been inactive for more than 30 minutes, the agent automatically starts a new session the next time you send a message. Your old session remains accessible via History.

## Tips for Getting the Best Answers

1. **Be specific** — "How do I add a notification topic preference for an investor?" works better than "How do notifications work?"
2. **Ask one thing at a time** — The agent handles single, focused questions more effectively than multi-part queries
3. **Mention the feature name** — If you know the feature (e.g. "capital calls", "investor KYC"), include it in your question
4. **Ask follow-ups** — The agent remembers the current conversation, so you can ask "Can you show me the exact steps?" after an initial answer
5. **Use the suggestions** — The sample questions on the welcome screen are designed to showcase what the agent can do well

## Frequently Asked Questions (FM)

### Navigation and getting there

**Where do I find the Customer Support Agent?**
Open your dashboard from the sidebar ([**Dashboard**](/entities/dashboard)) and look for the floating bubble in the bottom-right corner that reads **"Need help? Ask Customer Support Agent"**. Click the bubble or its blue robot button to open the chat panel. See [Opening the Chat](#opening-the-chat).

**I don't see the chat bubble on my dashboard. Why?**
The bubble only appears on the fund manager dashboard of a fund entity, and only after CapHive has set up the Customer Support Agent for your organisation with chat turned on. It is also hidden for CapHive support logins. If your colleagues see it and you do not, check you are on [**Dashboard**](/entities/dashboard); otherwise contact CapHive support.

**Is the support chat available on other pages?**
No, the Customer Support Agent bubble is only on the dashboard. Chat bubbles on stakeholder, portfolio or report pages are different assistants that answer questions about that data. For reading guides directly, use [**User Guides**](/user_guides) in the sidebar.

**Who in my organisation can use the support chat?**
Every fund manager user who lands on the fund manager dashboard can use it: company admins, operations staff and other employees. Investor (LP) logins see the investor dashboard instead, so they do not get this chat.

**How do I get back to a previous conversation?**
Open the chat panel and click **History** in its header. A sidebar lists your past sessions, newest first; click one to load its messages. See [Viewing Past Sessions](#viewing-past-sessions).

### Using the chat and managing conversations

**How do I ask the agent a question?**
Open the chat panel, type your question in plain English in the box at the bottom and press **Enter** or click **Send**. On a new session you can also click one of the suggested questions to fill it in. See [Asking Questions](#asking-questions).

**How do I start a fresh conversation?**
Click **New** in the chat panel header. Your current conversation is saved and stays available under **History**. See [Starting a New Conversation](#starting-a-new-conversation).

**How do I rate a response?**
Click the **Thumbs Up** or **Thumbs Down** icon under the agent's message. Rating is optional and takes one click. See [Rating Responses](#rating-responses).

**How do I tell the support team what was wrong with an answer?**
Click **Thumbs Down**, type a short note in the box that appears (for example "the steps are outdated") and click **Send**. The note is attached to that response for the support team. See [Providing Feedback Notes](#providing-feedback-notes).

**How do I ask to speak to a human?**
Tell the agent in the chat, for example "I want to talk to someone from support". The agent escalates the conversation with the reason **Customer Requested** and tells you it is handing the question to the support team. See [When the Agent Escalates to a Human](#when-the-agent-escalates-to-a-human).

**Can I make the chat panel bigger or move it?**
Yes. Click **Maximize** in the header for full screen, drag the blue header bar to move the panel, or drag its edges to resize it. Click **Maximize** again to restore the normal size.

**Can I delete a conversation?**
No. The chat panel has no delete option, so past sessions remain in **History**. Start a **New** conversation if you want a clean slate.

**How is the Customer Support Agent turned on for my organisation?**
CapHive creates the agent for your entity; it is not part of **Setup All Agents**. A company admin can then find it under [**Company menu → Agents**](/support_agents) and use **Edit** to control **Enabled** and **Enable Chat Assistant**, which decide whether the bubble shows.

### Why use the Customer Support Agent

**Why should I use the support chat instead of emailing support?**
It answers immediately, at any time, from the same user guides the support team uses, and usually includes the exact menu path to follow. If it cannot help, it hands your question to the support team with the conversation attached, so you do not have to explain it again.

**What kinds of questions can I ask?**
"How do I…" questions about any CapHive feature, "Show me / how many…" questions about your organisation's live data (investors, funds, capital calls, KYC and more), and follow-ups on things you asked before. See [What the Agent Can Help With](#what-the-agent-can-help-with).

**What can the agent not do?**
It cannot create, update or delete records, give investment or market advice, or see other organisations' data. Engineering-level questions are escalated to the support team. See [What It Cannot Help With](#what-it-cannot-help-with).

### What happens when I…

**What happens when I send a question?**
The message joins your current session, or starts a new one if you have been idle for more than 30 minutes. The agent searches the guides and, when needed, runs read-only queries on your organisation's data, then replies; if enabled for your entity, the reply is also quality-scored in the background.

**What happens when I click Thumbs Down?**
A feedback record is created for that response and it appears in the support team's Feedback Queue for review, together with any note you add. Nobody is emailed; the support team works through the queue and uses it to fix guides or agent behaviour.

**What happens when I click Thumbs Up?**
Your positive rating is saved against the response. It does not go to the support team's queue unless the automatic quality score for that answer is low.

**What happens when the agent escalates my question?**
An escalation record is created, your session is marked as escalated, and an email with your question and recent messages goes to the CapHive support team members assigned to your organisation. You can keep chatting; each reply starts with a note that your earlier query is being reviewed.

**What happens when the support team resolves my escalation?**
Your session returns to normal and the "has been escalated" note stops appearing in replies. The platform does not email you automatically; the support team contacts you directly if your question needs an answer from them.

**What happens when I start a new conversation?**
The old session is saved in **History** and the agent starts without its context. It can still search your past conversations when a question refers back to them.

### Troubleshooting and edge cases

**Is the agent available 24/7?**
Yes. The agent is always available as long as the CapHive platform is running. However, if the agent escalates to a human, the support team's response time depends on their working hours.

**Can the agent see my personal data?**
The agent can read your organisation's data in CapHive (investors, funds, capital calls and so on) to answer data questions, scoped to your entity as a whole. It cannot see other organisations' data or anything outside CapHive.

**What if the agent gives a wrong answer?**
Click **Thumbs Down** and leave a feedback note. This flags the response for review by the support team, who use it to correct the agent's knowledge.

**Can I use the agent on my phone?**
The chat bubble is part of the web dashboard, so use it from a browser. CapHive also has a WhatsApp channel for chat assistants, which only works where CapHive has set it up for your organisation; ask CapHive support whether it is available to you.

**Does the agent remember previous conversations?**
The agent remembers messages within the current session and can search your past sessions when relevant. It does not carry the full context of old sessions into a new one.

**Can I go back to a previous conversation?**
Yes. Click **History** in the chat header to see and switch between past sessions.

**The escalation note keeps appearing in every reply. How do I stop it?**
The note shows until the support team resolves the escalation on their side. It belongs to that one session, so clicking **New** starts a conversation without it while the escalated question is still being handled.

**The agent described a menu or button I cannot see. What should I do?**
Some menus depend on your role or on features enabled for your organisation, so the item may be hidden for you. Click **Thumbs Down** and note what you see instead; the support team uses this to correct the guide.

**The agent started a new session on its own. Why?**
Sessions expire after 30 minutes without a message, so your next question opens a fresh session. The earlier conversation is still in **History**. See [Session Auto-Expire](#session-auto-expire).

## Support Team Overview

The CS Feedback Review Dashboard is the central hub for the CapHive support team to monitor and maintain the quality of the Customer Support Agent. It provides:

- **Feedback Queue** — Agent responses that received negative user ratings or low automated quality scores
- **Escalation Queue** — Conversations where the agent handed off to a human because it could not answer

The dashboard is designed to be a single queue for all support activity — both quality monitoring and human escalation handling happen in one place.

### Your Role

As a support team member, you use this dashboard to:

1. **Review flagged responses** — Understand why a response was flagged (user complaint or low quality score) and decide what action to take
2. **Handle escalations** — Read the conversation context when the agent escalated, and resolve the user's issue
3. **Re-test questions** — After improving guides or agent behaviour, replay the original question to verify the agent now gives a better answer
4. **Track patterns** — Spot recurring issues, missing guide content, or areas where the agent consistently struggles

---

## Accessing the CS Feedback Review Dashboard

1. Log in to CapHive with your **support team account**
2. In the left sidebar, click **CS Reviews**

Alternatively, open the [CS Feedback Review dashboard](/cs_feedback_reviews) from this link.

### Access Requirements

- You must have the **support** role in CapHive
- You will only see feedback and escalations for entities (clients) you are **mapped to** via Support Client Mapping (see [Entity Scoping and Client Mappings](#entity-scoping-and-client-mappings))

## The Feedback Queue

The Feedback Queue shows agent responses that need attention. An item appears here when:

- A user gave the response a **negative rating** (thumbs down), OR
- The automated **LLM judge scored the response below 5 out of 9**

High-quality responses with positive or no ratings do not clutter this queue — only items that need review appear.

### Feedback Queue Columns

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Entity** | The client organisation where this conversation happened |
| **User** | The FM user who asked the question |
| **Question** | The user's original question (truncated; hover to see full text) |
| **Rating** | User's rating: Negative (red badge), Positive (green badge), or dash (no rating) |
| **Judge Score** | Automated quality score out of 9. Scores of 4 or below are highlighted in red |
| **Status** | Current review status (Open, Needs Review, Resolved, Dismissed, Pending Engineering) |
| **Date** | When the feedback was created |
| **Action** | "View" button to open the detailed review page |

### Status Meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Open** | New item, not yet reviewed |
| **Needs Review** | Automatically flagged by the judge (low score) — requires human review |
| **Resolved** | Reviewed and action taken (e.g. guide updated, response validated) |
| **Dismissed** | Reviewed and determined to not need action (e.g. user error, irrelevant feedback) |
| **Pending Engineering** | Requires a platform change or bug fix — handed off to the engineering team |

## Understanding Judge Scores

Every response from the Customer Support Agent is automatically scored by an AI judge on three dimensions:

| Dimension | Score Range | What It Measures |
|-----------|------------|------------------|
| **Completeness** | 0–3 | Did the response fully answer the question? Were any steps or details missing? |
| **Grounding** | 0–3 | Was the response based on actual user guides or real data? Or did the agent make things up? |
| **Actionability** | 0–3 | Did the response tell the user exactly what to do? Were the steps clear and specific? |
| **Total** | 0–9 | Sum of the three scores |

### Score Interpretation

| Total Score | Interpretation | Action Needed |
|-------------|---------------|---------------|
| **7–9** | Good to excellent response | No action needed unless user flagged it |
| **5–6** | Acceptable but could be better | Review if time permits; consider guide improvements |
| **3–4** | Poor response | Review promptly; likely missing guide content or incorrect information |
| **0–2** | Very poor response | Review immediately; may indicate a systematic issue |

### Issues Flagged

The judge may also flag specific issues (shown as a bullet list under the scores). Common issues include:
- Missing steps in a procedure
- Information not grounded in any source
- Vague or generic advice instead of specific instructions
- Incorrect feature names or navigation paths

---

## Taking Actions on Feedback

At the bottom of the right column on the detail page, you will find the **Actions** card (only shown for items that are not yet resolved or dismissed).

### Steps to Take Action

1. **Read the conversation** — Understand the user's question and the agent's response
2. **Check the judge scores** — See where the response fell short
3. **Review the tool calls** — Understand what sources the agent used
4. **Write resolution notes** — This field is required. Describe what action you took or why you chose this status. For example:
   - "Updated the KYC guide section 3.2 with PAN resubmission steps"
   - "Response was correct — user misunderstood the navigation"
   - "Bug in capital call date picker — forwarded to engineering"
5. **Choose an action:**

| Action | When to Use |
|--------|-------------|
| **Resolve** | You have addressed the issue (updated a guide, confirmed the response was correct, contacted the user). Also resolves any open escalation on the same chat session |
| **Dismiss** | The flag is not actionable (user error, irrelevant feedback, duplicate) |
| **Pending Engineering** | The issue requires a code change, bug fix, or platform improvement — it cannot be fixed by updating guides |

A confirmation dialog will appear before each action is applied.

### Best Practices for Resolution Notes

- Be specific about what you changed: "Added section on notification preferences to the investor_access_tutorial.md guide"
- If dismissing, explain why: "User asked about a third-party integration not related to CapHive"
- If marking as pending engineering, describe the expected fix: "Agent is returning stale data for capital call amounts — needs cache invalidation fix"

## Re-testing a Question

After you have improved a guide or made changes that should improve the agent's answer, you can **replay the original question** through the agent to verify the improvement.

### How to Re-test

1. Open the detail page for a feedback item
2. Scroll down to the **Re-test** card (below the tool calls section in the left column)
3. Click **"Re-test this question"**
4. A confirmation dialog appears: "This will run the question through the agent again. Proceed?"
5. Click **OK**

### What Happens

- The agent receives the exact same question in a **new session**
- It processes the question using its current knowledge (including any guide updates you made)
- The new response appears directly on the page, below the Re-test heading
- You can compare the new response side-by-side with the original response above

### When to Re-test

- After updating a user guide that was missing information
- After correcting a guide that had outdated steps
- After verifying that a data issue has been fixed
- Before resolving a feedback item, to confirm the improvement

### Important Notes

- Re-testing creates a new chat session under your support account — it does not affect the original user's session
- The re-test uses the **current state** of the guides and data, so it reflects any changes you have made since the original response
- If the re-test still produces a poor answer, you may need to make further guide improvements before resolving the item

## Handling Escalations

When the agent escalates a conversation, the FM user can **continue chatting** in the same session. The agent keeps answering new questions, but each response includes a note reminding the user that their escalated query is being reviewed by the support team.

### Escalation Detail Page

Clicking **"View"** on an escalation row opens a dedicated escalation detail page (not the feedback review page). This page shows:

**Left column — Conversation at escalation point:**
- The last 2 messages (the user's escalation-triggering question and the agent's response) at the time the escalation was created

**Right column — Escalation details:**
- Entity name, user name and email
- Escalation reason (Customer Requested, Low Confidence, Complex Issue, or Max Iterations)
- Summary of the issue (written by the agent)
- Current status (Open, Resolved, or Dismissed)
- Resolve and Dismiss action buttons (shown only for open escalations)

### Escalation Workflow

1. **Escalation appears** in the Escalation Queue with status "Open"
2. **Review the context** — Click "View" to open the escalation detail page and see the conversation at the point of escalation
3. **Take action** — This may involve:
   - Reaching out to the user via email to answer their question directly
   - Updating a guide so the agent can handle similar questions in future
   - Investigating a platform issue if the question revealed a bug
4. **Resolve the escalation** — On the detail page, click "Resolve". A confirmation dialog will appear.
5. **Session updated** — The session status changes back to "active" and the escalation notice no longer appears in the user's responses

### If the Escalation is Not Actionable

If the escalation was triggered inappropriately (e.g. the user was testing the system, or the question was outside the platform's scope), you can **Dismiss** it on the escalation detail page. A confirmation dialog will appear before the action is applied. Dismissing does not reset the chat session: it stays marked as escalated and the user keeps seeing the escalation note, so use **Resolve** if the note should stop.

## Entity Scoping and Client Mappings

The dashboard respects your **Support Client Mapping** — you will only see feedback and escalations for the entities (clients) you are assigned to.

### How It Works

- Each support team member is mapped to one or more client entities via Support Client Mapping
- The Entity dropdown in the filters only shows your mapped entities
- All feedback and escalation data is scoped to those entities

### If You Cannot See Expected Data

- Verify your Support Client Mapping is **enabled** (check with your admin under Support Mapping in the sidebar)
- Verify the mapping has not expired (mappings have an end date)
- Verify the entity has a Customer Support Agent configured and enabled

## Frequently Asked Questions (Support Team)

### Navigation and getting there

**How do I open the CS Feedback Review Dashboard?**
Log in with your support account and click [**CS Reviews**](/cs_feedback_reviews) in the left sidebar. The **Feedback Queue** tab opens first; switch to **Escalation Queue** for escalations. See [Accessing the CS Feedback Review Dashboard](#accessing-the-cs-feedback-review-dashboard).

**I don't see CS Reviews in my sidebar. Why?**
The link is only shown to users with the CapHive **support** role, and the dashboard refuses everyone else. Ask a CapHive super-admin to give your account the support role.

**How do I open a single feedback item or escalation?**
Click **View** on its row. Feedback items open their review page and escalations open their own detail page. See [Reviewing a Feedback Item](#reviewing-a-feedback-item) and [Escalation Detail Page](#escalation-detail-page).

**Where are Support Client Mappings managed?**
Super-admins manage them from [**Support Mapping**](/support_client_mappings) in the sidebar. A mapping must be enabled and not past its end date for you to see that client's items. See [Entity Scoping and Client Mappings](#entity-scoping-and-client-mappings).

**Where do I turn the automatic judge on or off for a client?**
Open the client entity's **Company Profile**, click **Edit**, and use the **CS Agent — Response Quality Judge** section: **Enable LLM-as-Judge** and **Judge Model**. The judge is on by default for every entity.

### Working the queues

**How do I resolve, dismiss or park a feedback item?**
Open the item, type your **Notes (required)** in the **Actions** card and click **Resolve**, **Dismiss** or **Pending Engineering**, then confirm. See [Taking Actions on Feedback](#taking-actions-on-feedback).

**How do I resolve or dismiss an escalation?**
Open the escalation with **View** on the **Escalation Queue** tab and click **Resolve** or **Dismiss**, then confirm. Both buttons only show while the escalation is **Open**. See [Escalation Workflow](#escalation-workflow).

**How do I re-test a question after fixing a guide?**
Open the feedback item, scroll to the **Re-test** card and click **Re-test this question**, then confirm. See [Re-testing a Question](#re-testing-a-question).

**How do I focus on one client or on urgent items?**
Use the filters bar: pick the client in **Entity**, set **Feedback Status**, **Rating**, **Judge Score ≤** or **Escalation Status**, and click **Filter**. See [Using Filters](#using-filters) for ready-made combinations.

**Can I reopen a feedback item I resolved or dismissed by mistake?**
Not from the dashboard. The **Actions** card is hidden once an item is **Resolved** or **Dismissed**, while **Pending Engineering** items keep it so they can be resolved later. Ask engineering if a status really has to be reverted.

### Why the review workflow matters

**Why should the support team work these queues?**
Negative ratings and low judge scores are the main signal that a guide is missing, outdated or unclear. Working the queue fixes the source so the agent answers the next client correctly, instead of the same question failing again.

**Why re-test before resolving?**
It proves the guide change actually fixed the answer, using the current guides and data. If the re-test still gives a poor answer, keep the item open and improve the guide further.

### What happens when I…

**What happens when an escalation is created?**
A record appears in the **Escalation Queue** as **Open**, the user's session is marked escalated, and an email titled "[CS Escalation] <client> — <reason>" with the last 10 messages goes to support users with an enabled mapping to that client. If no one is mapped, it goes to the CapHive support mailbox.

**What happens when I resolve a feedback item?**
The item is marked **Resolved** with your name and notes, and any open escalation on the same chat session is resolved too, which clears the escalation note for the user. The user is not notified, and the item cannot be reopened from the dashboard.

**What happens when I dismiss a feedback item or mark it Pending Engineering?**
Only the item's status, your name and your notes change; no ticket is created and no one is notified. A linked escalation stays open, so handle it separately.

**What happens when I resolve an escalation?**
The linked chat session changes from "escalated" back to "active", and the escalation note no longer appears in the user's responses. Your name and the resolution time are recorded for audit; the user is not emailed.

**What happens when I dismiss an escalation?**
The escalation is marked **Dismissed** and leaves the open list, but the user's session stays marked as escalated, so their replies in that session keep showing the escalation note. Use **Resolve** if the user should stop seeing it.

**What happens when I re-test a question?**
The original question is run through the agent again under your support account, and the new answer appears on the page for comparison. The item's status does not change and the original user's session is untouched.

### Troubleshooting and edge cases

**How quickly should I respond to escalations?**
Although the user can keep chatting with the agent, the escalated issue stays unresolved until you act on it. Aim to review open escalations within the same business day.

**Can I see conversations from entities I am not mapped to?**
No. The dashboard is strictly scoped to your enabled mappings. If you need another entity's data, ask your admin to update your Support Client Mapping.

**How does the automated judge work?**
When the judge is enabled for the entity, every agent response is scored in the background by a separate AI model on completeness, grounding and actionability (0–3 each, 9 total). Responses scoring below 5 are marked **Needs Review** and appear in the Feedback Queue. See [Understanding Judge Scores](#understanding-judge-scores).

**Can I change the judge scoring threshold?**
No, the below-5 threshold is fixed at the platform level. What you can change per entity is whether the judge runs and which model it uses, on the entity's **Edit** page.

**What should I do if I see the same question failing repeatedly?**
This usually means a gap in the user guides. Check which guide files the agent searched on the detail page and whether they contain the answer; if not, update the guide and use **Re-test** to confirm the fix.

**Can FM users see the judge scores or my resolution notes?**
No. Judge scores, resolution notes and the review dashboard are only visible to support team members. FM users only see their own chat conversations and the thumbs up/down controls.

**Does the re-test affect the original user's experience?**
No. The re-test runs under your support account, not the user's, so the original conversation and session are untouched.

**What is "Pending Engineering" status for?**
Use it when the issue cannot be fixed by updating guides alone and needs a code change, bug fix or new feature. It keeps the item visible so support-driven engineering issues can be tracked and resolved later.

**Why is an item with a positive rating in the Feedback Queue?**
Items enter the queue on a negative rating or a judge score below 5. A thumbs-up answer that the judge scored low still needs a human check.

**Re-test says "Could not find the original user question". What now?**
The re-test needs the user's question that came before the flagged response in the same session, and it could not be found. Review the item from its conversation context and resolve it without a re-test.
