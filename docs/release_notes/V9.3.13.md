# Release Notes — V9.3.13
**Released: 08 November 2026**

---

## What's New

### Compliance Monitoring — every fund checked against its rules nightly
A new **Compliance** area (Company menu → Compliance, and a Compliance tab on each fund) checks your funds every night against rule packs. You can attach CapHive's regulator packs (SEBI — AIF and IFSCA — Fund Management) or your own fund-agreement rules. Each result shows its working step by step, for example "₹72.00 cr ÷ ₹270.00 cr = 26.67% > 25% — Breach". A breach opens one item in the register and stays open until a later run passes. Duties that data cannot prove, such as a quarterly SEBI report being filed, are tracked with due dates and evidence. Any change that weakens monitoring needs a second person to approve it, for example explaining a breach or relaxing a limit.

### Compliance Monitoring — AI reads documents and drafts checks
When switched on, AI reads figures from your documents, such as the approved amount on an IC note. A figure counts in a check only after a person confirms it against its quote and page. On your own rule packs, **Describe in words** drafts a check from a sentence, which you can correct and test on a fund before saving.

### Compliance Monitoring — a guided start and a morning digest
A **Getting started** panel takes you through eight setup steps and ticks each one off from your own data. Every compliance page has a **What's next** box with up to three things you can do now. A morning digest email tells the people you choose what changed overnight.

### Dashboards — 17 new insight widgets
You can now add new read-only widgets to company, portfolio, fund, investor and operations dashboards. They include KPI Movers, KPI Threshold Watch, Monitoring Status, Valuation Freshness, Winners & Losers, Sector Exposure, Remittance Ageing, NAV per Unit Trend, Hedge Expiries, Investor Pending Actions, Side-letter Obligations, Needs Attention, Overdue Tasks by Assignee and Stuck Workflows. Each widget has a one-line explanation, and columns with a non-obvious meaning have tooltips. Investors never see these widgets.

### Dashboards — change a widget's settings without leaving the page
A settings icon on each widget opens a side panel with its settings and size. When you save, only that widget reloads, and your fund and company filters stay as they were.

### Tables — change columns and custom fields where you see them
A columns button beside a list's search box opens a side panel. In it you can reorder, rename, add and remove columns, set their type and alignment, or reset the list to its defaults. A form icon on a record page's fields card, and in a form's header, does the same for that record type's custom fields. You can also mark fields Required, Read only or Internal and add help text there.

### Custom form editor — one field at a time, grouped by step
The form type edit page now lists your fields on the left, grouped by wizard step. You can search them, drag them to reorder, or drop one on another step. On the right you see one field's settings in four short cards, with a live preview and one Save bar that counts your unsaved changes. A field's condition reads as a sentence, such as "Only when Investor type is Individual". If you try to leave with unsaved changes, the page asks first.

### Report Builder — easier to build and to trust
- **New report** is now one menu with ten starter templates and a Duplicate option.
- The builder shows a live preview. Every tab opens with a help box, and complex settings have tooltips.
- **Describe it** lets AI draft a report from a question. It lists anything it could not do.
- Exports show their progress until the file is ready to download, and recent exports are kept.
- Scheduled reports show the time zone, when they were last sent and when they will go next, and can send a test.
- Totals and number formats are now consistent across reports.

### AI Assistants — one chat layout everywhere
The Assistants, the analysis chat on a record and the floating **Need help?** window now look and work the same way. Earlier conversations are listed on the left with a search box. Answers come in cards with tables and a Copy button. Suggested follow-up questions have an **Ask** button that sends them straight away. When it helps, an answer can include a verdict banner, a list of checks or numbered next steps.

### AI Slides and PowerPoint export — clean, readable decks
AI Slides now builds 5–8 slides from your conversation, each with an icon, its main point and a "What this means" line. It uses only numbers from the conversation, with no filler text. **Export PowerPoint** no longer draws text on top of other text. Long answers carry on onto extra slides, and long tables repeat their header row.

### Zoho eSign — reminders, deadlines and your own Zoho account
Companies can now send documents for signature through Zoho Sign. You can set how often signers are reminded, send a reminder yourself, or turn reminders off. You can also extend a document's signing deadline. The Signature Certificate is saved with the signed copy. A company admin can connect the company's own Zoho Sign account, in any Zoho region. From then on, new documents go through that account. Documents already sent finish on the account they were sent from.

### Sign Digio documents without leaving CapHive
A logged-in signer can sign a Digio document from the document page with **Sign now**. They don't need to open the email link, although the email link still works. When a document uses a signing order, only the signer whose turn it is sees the button. Digio's audit trail is now saved next to the signed document. This is off by default. Ask CapHive to switch it on for your company.

## Improvements

### Investments page — upload and download options in one Actions menu
The Investments header used to show up to seven buttons. It now has **New Investment** and an **Actions** menu that holds the upload, view and download options.

### Tables on a phone keep the record's name
On a narrow screen, tables now keep the first column, usually the record's name, and fold the action buttons and middle columns behind the expander.

### Commitment Account Entries tab — filter without leaving the tab
**Filter** on a commitment's Account Entries tab now opens inside the tab, already limited to that folio and its reporting date. **Delete Results** is hidden on this tab, because it would have deleted matching entries for every folio in the fund. **Show All** opens the full list for the fund.

### Commitment Ratios tab — charts show the commitment's own ratios
The charts on a commitment's Ratios tab used to plot the fund's ratios. They now plot the commitment's own. If the commitment has no ratios yet, the tab tells you how to compute them.

### Portfolio dashboard widgets — settings that work as labelled
- Portfolio Stats fields are now picked from a list, and a renamed label shows on the next page load.
- The company widget's Default currency setting now works.
- Settings that never had any effect have been removed from the form.
- Summarise Notes names the dates it covers and confirms where the summary will appear.

### Security improvements across the platform
A full security review led to many fixes to permissions and data separation between firms. Changes you may notice:
- **Forgot password** always replies "If your email exists…".
- Ten wrong two-factor codes within an hour lock the account and send an unlock email.
- A deactivated user's API and AI connector access stops at once.
- After you change your password, connected AI tools ask you to sign in again.

### Faster pages
Dozens of pages that loaded their data one row at a time now load it in a few queries. These include NAV lists, evergreen unit registers, workflow templates, Document Checks, the dealing tracker, kanban boards and dashboard fund cards.

## Bug Fixes

### Secondary sale download sheet works again
Downloading a secondary sale's sheet failed with an error for every user. It now downloads, with the buyer and allocation columns filled in.

### Downloads of documents with ₹, dashes or Japanese in the name
A document whose name contained characters such as ₹, an em dash or Japanese text could not be opened or downloaded. It now downloads with its full name.

### Assistant conversations no longer get stuck
A question to the Portfolio Company Assistant could end with "Not answered", and every later question in that conversation then failed too. The assistant now answers, and conversations that were stuck recover.

### AI Slides opens again
Opening AI Slides from an assistant chat showed an error page. It now opens your deck.

### Kanban cards keep their order
Cards with the same position in a kanban column could swap places when the page reloaded. They now stay in a fixed order.
