# Release Notes — V9.3.06
**Released: 21 October 2026**

---

## What's New

### Investor app — one app, several fund managers and regions
Investors who work with more than one fund manager, or in more than one region, can now move between them inside the same CapHive app.

- **One fund manager at a time.** Home, Documents and Alerts show a single fund manager. A small caption at the top lets you switch, and the app remembers your last choice.
- **Fund manager branding.** Home shows the logo of the fund manager you are viewing.
- **Switch region from Profile.** If your login covers two or more regions, you can switch without signing in again. Signing out ends every region's session.

### Investor app — a fuller fund page
The fund screen now shows your totals and a Performance card with the headline ratios, followed by sections for Capital calls, Distributions, Portfolio, Units and Documents. Each section has a one-line summary and its own screen.

- **Switches respected.** Portfolio, Units and Performance appear only when the fund manager has turned them on for investors.
- **Ratios.** Your folio's ratios are shown above the fund's.
- **Payments received.** A capital call lists your payments, with the proof the fund manager attached.
- **Distribution details.** Tap a distribution to see the net amount payable, whether it is paid or scheduled, the breakdown of gross, tax and deductions, and a link to the distribution notice.

### Investor app — approvals and KYC
- **Approvals.** Items waiting for your answer appear in your To do list, and you can answer them in the app. Approvals that need extra details open the website form.
- **KYC.** **Profile → KYC** lists your KYC records and their status. **Complete KYC** opens the website form, and fund managers can ask investors for updates.

### Email is fixed; phone number changes need a code
- **Email can no longer be changed** once a user exists, from any screen or integration.
- **Changing or removing an existing mobile number** now needs a 6-digit code sent to the user's email. It expires after 10 minutes, and requests are limited. Use **Change phone** on the user's page. Adding a first number needs no code.
- A confirmation email is sent after the number changes.

---

## Improvements

### Tab labels stay on one line in the app
Long labels such as Distributions and Documents no longer break mid-word on small phones or with large text sizes.
