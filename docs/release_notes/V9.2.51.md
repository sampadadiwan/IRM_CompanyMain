# Release Notes — V9.2.51

**Released: 3 June 2026**

---

## What's New

### Deal Management Agent — AI-powered deal pipeline tracking

A new intelligent support agent is now available to help manage deal activity. The agent monitors your deal pipeline, generates periodic digest reports with activity summaries and next steps, tracks deal state transitions in your kanban board, and surfaces priority updates so your team stays aligned on portfolio progress.

### Enhanced Formula & Account Entry Explanations — AI-powered clarity

Your fund's formulas and account entries now come with detailed AI-generated explanations that break down complex calculations into clear, understandable steps. The system automatically extracts the key logic behind each formula and highlights important assumptions, making it easier for stakeholders to understand financial mechanics.

### Fund Ratio Templates & Multi-Fund Comparison — Standardized reporting

Fund ratio runs can now be generated from templates, allowing you to standardize how you compare metrics across multiple funds or portfolio companies. Templates support flexible filtering by portfolio company, enabling consistent multi-fund ratio analysis.

### Dropbox Sign Integration — More e-signing choices

In addition to DocuSign, your team can now use Dropbox Sign as a third-party e-signature provider for documents that require signing.

---

## Improvements

### Portfolio Company Filtering — Smarter selection logic

Fund ratio runs now have enhanced portfolio company resolution with comprehensive filtering options, making it easier to run consistent ratios across different portfolio scenarios.

### Capital Call Approvals — Automatic document generation

Capital call approval workflows now automatically generate required documents as part of the approval process, reducing manual steps.

### Weekly Email Reports — New digest task

A new background task has been added for generating weekly email reports with both internal and external rendering options.

### Better Formula Breakdown Display — Clearer financial calculations

Formula explanations now correctly identify result keys, expose formula addends, and handle multi-statement expressions with improved clarity. The AI prompt has been enhanced to provide more accurate breakdowns and reduce noise.

### Cross-Portfolio Ratio Runs — Unified metrics across funds

You can now generate ratio runs across multiple funds using shared templates, ensuring consistent metric tracking across your entire portfolio.
