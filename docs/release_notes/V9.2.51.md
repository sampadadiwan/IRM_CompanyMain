# Release Notes — V9.2.51

**Released: 3 June 2026**

---

## What's New

### Enhanced Distribution Amount Tracking — More financial details at your fingertips

Distribution notices now capture and display net and gross payable amounts, income, and reinvestment figures. This gives your team complete visibility into financial movements across distribution payments.

### Improved Audit Trail for Distributions — Track every change with confidence

Distribution notices now generate detailed audit records with audit names for each account entry, making it easier to reconcile and review distribution adjustments over time.

### Multi-Level Account Entry Breakdowns — Detailed visibility across your fund structure

Account entry breakdowns now work seamlessly at fund, KYC, and folio levels. See exactly how amounts are broken down across your investment structure with consistent naming conventions (both standard and audit names).

---

## Improvements

### Account entry details now consistently available across fund levels

Fund template decorators now properly fetch and display account entry details whether you're reviewing at fund, KYC, or folio level.

### Folio-level KYC account entry data now included in reports

KYC account entries are now fetched and included at the folio level, providing more complete financial records in your distribution documents.
