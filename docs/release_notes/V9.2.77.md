# Release Notes — V9.2.77
**Released: 20 July 2026**

---

## What's New

### Net-payable-before-tax figure added to Distribution templates
Fund Term Distribution templates now include a net-payable-before-tax figure (the payable amount with tax added back in) for LPs, GPs, individual investors, and KYC groups, across prior, current, and combined periods, with percentage breakdowns. It sits alongside the existing net payable and income figures so you can see distribution amounts both before and after tax.
