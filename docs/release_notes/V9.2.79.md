# Release Notes — V9.2.79
**Released: 23 July 2026**

---

## What's New

### Deal access rights now respect view-only permissions
Team members with view-only access to a deal can now see notes, tasks, and deal activity status without being able to change them. Update buttons and status controls are automatically hidden for anyone who doesn't have edit rights, so read-only reviewers can no longer accidentally change a deal's status or its steps.

---

## Improvements

### Cleaner, more consistent grids across the platform
Tables using the AG Grid view (deals, portfolio investments, and related lists) now size and paginate more reliably. Page numbers no longer wrap awkwardly onto two lines, and grid rows no longer get cut off at the top or bottom of the screen.

### More accurate distribution figures on capital distribution and remittance documents
Fund Term Distribution reports, Capital Distribution Payment documents, and Capital Remittance documents now use the most up-to-date accrual entry for each investor's holding and correctly account for KYC-verified commitments as of the relevant date. This gives a more accurate and consistent view of distribution amounts across all generated documents.

---

## What to Do

| If you use...                              | Action                                                                                     |
|---------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Deals with mixed team access levels          | No action needed. View-only team members will automatically see a read-only version of deal activities. |
| Deal, portfolio, or investment grids         | No action needed. Grid display issues are resolved automatically.                          |
| Fund Term Distribution or distribution/remittance documents | No action needed. Figures now reflect the corrected calculations automatically.            |
