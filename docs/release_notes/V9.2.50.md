# Release Notes — V9.2.50
**Released: 30 May 2026**

---

## What's New

### Portfolio Reports — Auto-Generate, Export to Word and PowerPoint
Portfolio report drafts can now be generated automatically on a schedule — daily, weekly, or monthly — so your team always has a fresh draft ready without manual kicks. Reports can be exported to Word or PowerPoint, with placeholders for company name, fund name, and KPI tables automatically filled in from your data. You can also bulk-generate drafts across multiple portfolio companies at once, and request AI-suggested section outlines for any report.

### KYC Onboarding — Multi-Step Form with Inline Editing
The KYC onboarding form has been redesigned as a structured, nine-step flow that guides investors through each section clearly. Operations teams can now edit the form's step labels and field content inline — no need to leave the page — and changes are reflected immediately in the investor-facing view.

### Commitment Adjustments — Download as Spreadsheet
Commitment adjustment records can now be downloaded as an Excel file directly from the adjustments view. This makes it straightforward to share or audit adjustment history outside the platform.

### Deals — Filter by Activity Status and Update from the Grid
The deals index now includes a filter panel so you can narrow the list by activity status, investor, or other criteria. Activity statuses can also be updated directly from the grid using a floating menu, without opening the full deal record.

---

## Improvements

### Fund Documents — "As Of Date" Now Captured
When processing fund documents, the system now reads and stores an "as of date" where one is present in the document. This gives your team a reliable date reference on the document record without manual entry.

### KYC Forms — Custom Field Types for Specific Answers
KYC form fields can now be typed (for example, as a date, numeric value, or free text), allowing the platform to handle and validate custom answers more precisely during AI-assisted extraction.

### KYC Document Upload — Clearer Prompts and Support Agent Mode
Upload prompts during KYC onboarding have been standardised for clarity. A new support-agent mode is also available, which adjusts how document handling instructions are presented when a team member is assisting an investor through the process.

### Signed Document Checks — More Reliable Status Detection
The logic that determines whether a document has been signed — rather than just generated or approved — has been tightened. Edge cases where a signed document could be misclassified are now handled correctly.
