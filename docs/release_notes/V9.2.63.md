# Release Notes — V9.2.63
**Released: 22 June 2026**

---

## What's New

### RTA Reconciliation Agent — automatically check unit statements against your records

You can now upload a unit statement PDF from your RTA (Registrar and Transfer Agent, e.g. CAMS or KFintech) and have it automatically checked against your fund's own unit records. The agent flags mismatches at both the scheme and individual folio level, highlights folios that appear on the statement but not in your records (and vice versa), and tracks coverage so you can see at a glance whether every scheme your fund holds was actually included in the statement. Reports are saved to a history tab on the agent's page and can optionally be emailed after each run.

### Changing a stakeholder's email now safely moves their records along with it

When you update a stakeholder's primary email to one that belongs to a different profile, you'll see a confirmation screen explaining what will happen before you confirm: their deal participations, portal access permissions, and notice delivery settings move to the new profile, and any user account that belongs exclusively to them follows along too, so they don't lose portal access. If no profile exists yet for the new email, one is created automatically.

---

## Improvements

### Support agents are now grouped by category for easier browsing

Agents on the Support Agents page are now organized into clear groups — Onboarding, Finance, Ops, Portfolio, and Deal — making it faster to find the agent you're looking for.

### Agent enabled/disabled status is now shown consistently everywhere

The "Enabled" or "Disabled" badge on a support agent's card and detail page now always reflects the same setting, so the status you see on the agent list matches what you see when you open it.

### User guides added for three more agents

In-app help guides are now available for the Portfolio Scoring Agent, the Investor Presentation Analyst, and the RTA Reconciliation Agent, covering what each one does, what it needs as input, and how to read its output.

### Fixed an occasional naming conflict when creating new entities

Newly created entities (such as investors or funds) with very similar names could occasionally fail to save due to an internal naming conflict. This has been resolved.
