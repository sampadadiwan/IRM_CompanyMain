# Release Notes — V9.2.60

**Released: 18 June 2026**

---

## What's New

### Meet your new AI Customer Support Agent

We've added a full-featured support assistant that your team can use to answer stakeholder questions 24/7. The agent can search your user guides, query fund and investor data, and review conversation history to give contextual answers. When a question is too complex, it escalates to a human team member with full context. Every response gets a thumbs-up/thumbs-down rating from users, and your support team has a dashboard to review and improve agent performance over time.

### Send ad-hoc notifications with documents to stakeholders

Fund commitment holders can now receive notifications with document attachments directly from the app — no need to email files separately. When setting up a notification, choose which stakeholders to notify and what documents to attach. They'll receive a message with everything they need in one place.

### Better feedback and escalation tracking for your support team

Support teams now have a centralized dashboard showing which support requests are still active, which ones have been escalated to humans, and how users have rated the AI's responses. This gives you visibility into what's working and where you need to step in.

---

## Improvements

### Smoother error messages in Turbo Streams

Flash messages now render correctly when pages update via Turbo Streams, instead of sometimes disappearing or showing stale content.

### LP Assistant now handles complex operations cleanly

The LP (Limited Partner) Assistant no longer returns errors when processing certain multi-step operations, thanks to improved handling of asynchronous step returns.

### More reliable validation on model names and lengths

Several models now have stricter but clearer validation rules to prevent silent truncation or unexpected errors, especially when dealing with longer names or values.
