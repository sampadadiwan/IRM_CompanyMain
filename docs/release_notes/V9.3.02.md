# Release Notes — V9.3.02
**Released: 5 October 2026**

---

## What's New

### Capital calls fill in live — remittances appear without a reload
When you generate remittances for a capital call, the page now updates itself. A **Generating
remittances… 120 of 800** line counts up while the work runs, and the remittances list and the
summary cards fill in as soon as it finishes, with a message telling you it's done. If you open the
call part-way through, you see how far it has got. A filter you've applied to the remittances list
stays in place through the refresh.

If some remittances can't be saved, a warning lists them. Remittances that already exist are counted
and left alone, never created twice.

### Clickable journey steps explain what to do and what happens next
Click any step in a journey's step bar and a popup tells you what to do, what happens when the step
completes (including whether investors will be emailed), why it matters, and who gets reminders.
The hover tip is shorter, with the completion date and message on separate lines.

---

## Improvements

### Journey buttons take you straight to the right place
On capital calls and distributions, **Create remittances** and **Create payments** for uploaded
data open the import page directly, and **Verify remittances** switches to the right tab and runs
Verify Pending Remittances for you. On a KYC, **Set up KYC email** opens the email itself in a new
tab.

### "Review email" is now "Review custom notification"
The step now names the email that will go out — your call or distribution's own notification if you
set one, otherwise the fund default. You can open it in a new tab or create a new one from the step.
Existing journeys and templates are renamed automatically.

### Clearer message after approving notices
Once you approve notices in bulk, or when none are left to approve, the page tells you the journey
will move on by itself and offers **Close this tab**.

### You're no longer emailed about your own action
Moving a journey forward yourself — marking a step reviewed, approving notices, or undoing a step —
no longer sends you a "Needs your review", "Action Required", "Complete" or "Your sign-off was
reset" email. Everyone else involved is still notified.

### Custom fields on later steps no longer disappear from single-page forms
On single-page forms such as Capital Commitment, a custom field set to Step 2 or later vanished from
the form, so its value couldn't be entered or edited. These forms now show all your custom fields.
Multi-step forms such as the Investor KYC form still show each step's fields on its own page.

### Very large allocations no longer stop background jobs
Running an allocation on a fund with around a million entries could freeze background processing
and cancel other jobs that were running at the time. Large allocations now complete without
interrupting anything else. Re-running an existing allocation still clears the earlier results
first.

## What to Do

| If you use...   | Action                              |
|------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Capital calls | No action needed. |
