# Release Notes — V9.2.78
**Released: 21 July 2026**

---

## Improvements

### More accurate Distribution Cash figures on Fund Term Distribution reports
Distribution Cash is now calculated using the net payable amount plus tax, instead of the gross payable amount minus reinvestment. This gives a more accurate picture of actual cash distributed for LPs, GPs, individual investors, and KYC groups.

### Recallable Distribution calculation corrected
Recallable Distribution now correctly subtracts the reinvestment amount when comparing against 20% of committed capital, instead of adding it. This ensures the recallable amount shown for LPs, GPs, individual investors, and KYC groups is calculated correctly.
