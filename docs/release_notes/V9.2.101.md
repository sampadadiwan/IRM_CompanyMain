# Release Notes — V9.2.101
**Released: 8 October 2026**

---

## What's New

### Date checks now cover capital calls, remittances, payments and commitments
V9.2.100 started refusing mistyped years (such as `0224` or `2204`) on valuations, portfolio investments, fund ratios, allocation runs and Investor Statements. The same check, by default **no more than 20 years before or 20 years after today**, now also applies to the dates behind investor money movements:

- **Capital calls**: call date and due date
- **Capital remittances**: remittance date
- **Remittance payments**: the date a payment was received
- **Capital commitments**: commitment date

A refused date shows a message that names it, for example: *"Capital call dates 2204-03-31 – 2204-04-14 must be within 20 years before and 20 years after today."* The form comes back with your entries kept, so you only need to fix the date.

The check runs whenever these records are saved: on the form, through imports, when a bank statement is applied, and when remittances are generated for a call. A refused capital call generates no remittances. In an import, only the row with the bad date fails.

### Approval and notification checks are unchanged
Capital calls and remittances already have checks such as "call notices pending approval". These still apply only when you **Approve** a call or **Send** a notification, exactly as before. They do not start blocking everyday edits such as recording a payment.

### Your existing records are left alone
Only new dates, and dates being changed, are checked. A remittance or commitment dated more than 20 years ago stays fully editable, and payments can still be recorded against it as long as the payment's own date is in the window.

### Foreign-currency payments show the date problem clearly
For an investor whose folio is in a different currency from the fund, a mistyped payment date used to fail with an exchange-rate error, or an error page. It now shows the date message, both on the payment form and when applying a bank statement.

### Set your own window, or switch the check off
Four new rules appear under **Entity → Validation Rules**: "Capital Call Date Out Of Range", "Capital Remittance Date Out Of Range", "Capital Remittance Payment Date Out Of Range" and "Capital Commitment Date Out Of Range". A company admin can change **past_years** and **future_years** (both default to 20), turn a rule into a warning, or switch it off, for your entity only. These forms show no warning pop-up, so a rule set to warning lets the save go ahead.

---

## What to Do

| If you use... | Action |
|---|---|
| Capital calls, remittances, payments or commitments | No action needed for dates within 20 years of today. If a date is refused, check it for a mistyped year. |
| Funds with capital calls dated more than 20 years ago | When you add a new investor to such a fund, the catch-up remittances for those old calls cannot be created. A company admin should first raise **past_years** (for example, to 30) on the **Capital Remittance Date Out Of Range** rule. |
| Funds holding genuinely older capital activity | A company admin raises **past_years** on the relevant rules, or switches them off, under **Entity → Validation Rules**. |
| Commitment adjustments | No change: an adjustment's offsetting payment is always created, whatever its date. |
| Deploying this release (engineering) | Run `bin/rails rules:seed_defaults` after the code deploy, separately on **IN** and on **US**. The new checks do nothing until the rules are seeded. To roll back without a deploy, switch the four rules off. |
