# Release Notes — V9.2.87
**Released: 20 August 2026**

---

## What's New

### Change your two-factor method — switch from WhatsApp to an app, or back

Until now, once you had chosen how to receive your sign-in codes you were stuck with it. You can now reset your method from your Profile page: click **Change 2FA Method**, confirm your password, and pick a fresh method — Authenticator app, WhatsApp or text message — the next time you sign in. Two-factor stays switched on the whole time, so your account is never left unprotected.

### Administrators can reset a colleague's two-factor method

If someone in your team has lost their phone or changed their number, an administrator can reset their method from the user's page. The person is emailed about the reset and simply chooses a new method at their next sign-in — no more being locked out until a developer steps in.

### Clear "what will happen" popup before any reset

Both the self-service and the administrator reset open a popup that spells out every consequence before you can confirm: the current method stops working, whether you will be signed out now or at the next sign-in, that two-factor stays on, that an email will be sent, and what to have ready before you continue. Administrators also get a reminder to check the request really came from the person concerned.

### Investor notices can now target the right people, with smarter links

Notices can be narrowed to a more specific group of investors on top of their usual audience, and any link inside a notice can now open straight to that reader's own dashboard instead of a generic page. Your CapHive team sets this up on request — nothing changes in how you view or dismiss notices.

---

## Improvements

### Sign-in screen explains why you are choosing a method again
After a reset, the method-selection screen shows when the reset happened and whether you or an administrator did it, so nobody is left wondering.

### Two-factor status now visible to administrators
The user page shows whether two-factor is on and which method is in use, so administrators can see the situation before resetting.

### Clearer, easier-to-use permission switches on the user profile screen
The on/off switches for a user's permissions and notification settings now have a cleaner toggle design with short explanations next to each one, including what the country code and phone number fields are used for. Investors no longer see permission options that only apply to your own team.

### Dates now match across emails, screens, and activity lists
Notification emails, allocation run and capital call summary cards, and the session activity list now all show dates in the same clear format, so there is no mismatch between what you see on screen and what arrives in your inbox.

### Filtering by date now works reliably no matter your date format
Fixed an issue where filtering a list by a date could silently find nothing if your browser's date convention (day/month vs. month/day) didn't match the app's. Date filters now use the same guided date picker used elsewhere in the app, removing the ambiguity.

### KYC reminder emails no longer reference a link that isn't there
Fixed an issue where a KYC reminder email could still say "this link is valid until…" even on reminders configured not to include a link.

### Document previews no longer fail to load
Fixed an issue where a document preview could show an error instead of the document itself.
