# Release Notes — V9.2.85
**Released: 13 August 2026**

---

## What's New

### LP Relationship CRM — track every contact at each investor
Stakeholder pages now double as a lightweight CRM. Every note and task can be tied to the specific person you spoke with, not just the firm, so each contact builds their own history of calls, emails, and follow-ups. A new Kanban board lets you drag tasks across stages, filter by owner or tag, and see everything at a glance for a deal, an investor, or your whole team.

### Deactivate an entity with one confirmed action
Support staff can now deactivate an entity from its page and see exactly what will happen before confirming: every active employee login is disabled, all approved investor access is revoked, and investor emails and notifications stop going out. Nothing is deleted — funds, documents, commitments, and history stay intact, and everything can be reactivated later.

### Side Letter Obligations — never miss a commitment again
A new agent reads the side letter attached to each commitment and pulls out every obligation — reporting requirements, fee terms, co-investment rights — with the exact clause it came from. Your legal team reviews and approves each one before it goes live; approved obligations then become tracked tasks with automatic reminders before, on, and after the due date, escalating to your team if something slips.

---

## Improvements

### Account Entry reports now respect exact date filters
Searching or reporting on Account Entries with a specific date, or only an end date, could return incomplete results because of a default date range applied behind the scenes. Date filters now apply exactly as entered in every case.

### Data quality reports no longer go to the wrong inboxes
If a Data Quality Agent had no recipient configured, its reports could be sent to every active employee at the entity by mistake. Reports are now only sent to configured recipients, and entities in sandbox mode correctly receive their reports at the sandbox address instead.

### A refreshed, faster sign-in experience
The sign-in page has a cleaner layout with collapsible sections for password and magic-link sign-in, smoother focus handling as you move between fields, and updated branding.
