# Release Notes — V9.2.48
**Released: 24 May 2026**

---

## What's New

### Note Summarizer — AI Summaries of Investor Notes
A new AI agent reads your investor meeting notes and produces a concise written summary for each investor. Summaries appear in a dedicated tab on the investor card and in a new dashboard widget, so your team can quickly review the key themes and action items without reading every raw note entry. The agent can be configured per fund and run on demand.

### Inline Editing — Edit Summaries and Scorecards Without Leaving the Page
Note summaries and scorecard entries can now be edited directly on the investor card — double-click any summary or score field to enter edit mode, make your change, and save without opening a separate form. Changes are reflected immediately on the page.

### KYC Send Workflow — Magic Links and Status Tracking
Fund admins can now send investors a personalised KYC form link directly from the KYC index. The investor receives an email with a secure one-time link that opens their KYC form without requiring a login. The system tracks whether the link was sent, when the investor started filling in the form, and whether they submitted it, giving your team full visibility over outstanding KYC requests from a single view. A **Pending KYC Requests** button on the index page filters to investors who have been sent a link but have not yet submitted.

### Document-First KYC — Upload an ID and Let AI Pre-Fill the Form
When an investor opens their KYC link, they can now upload their identity document first. An AI extraction step reads the document and pre-populates the KYC fields, reducing the manual data entry required before submission. Admins can review and correct the extracted values before the record is finalised.

### Distribution Fees — Copy Values Across Entries and Track Cumulative Amounts
The distribution fee section now supports a **Copy to All** button that pushes the values from one fee entry to all others in a single click — useful when the same fee applies across multiple account entries. You can also expand or collapse all account entries together. Two new fields — cumulative amount and entry type — give you finer control over how fees are categorised and rolled up.

---

## Improvements

### Custom Form Fields — Checkboxes Now Visible on the Detail View
Checkbox fields defined in custom forms are now displayed correctly when viewing a completed form record. Previously, checkbox values were not rendered on the show page.

### KYC Document Upload — More Reliable Email Notifications
Email notification checks during the document upload step have been tightened so that notifications fire correctly in edge cases where they were previously skipped.

### KPI and KYC Reminder Modals — Refreshed Visual Style
The reminder modals used for KPI report reminders and KYC update requests have been updated to match the current modal design across the platform. Behaviour is unchanged.

## What to Do

| If you use...                          | Action                                                                                            |
|----------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Investor KYC management                | Use **Pending KYC Requests** to track investors who haven't submitted after receiving a link.     |
