# Release Notes — V9.2.97
**Released: 20 September 2026**

---

## What's New

### Type dates directly into any date field or filter
Dates no longer have to be picked from a calendar. Click into a date field on a filter form, or into a date column filter on a grid, and type the date in your own format — the calendar icon is still there if you prefer it. Grid column filters previously showed a bare calendar box that could not be typed into and whose day/month order followed your browser rather than the app, so a grid could read 08/09 as September 8 while the rows above it read 8 August. Typed dates are accepted with loose separators (1-2-2026, 1.2.2026), and an impossible date such as 31/02/2026 is now flagged instead of quietly becoming 2 March. Date filters on ad-hoc report grids, which never matched anything at all, now work.

### Payment imports warn when Verified could not be applied
Uploading remittance payments with Verified = Yes against a capital call that has not been approved used to report a completely successful import, while silently leaving every remittance unverified — collected totals stayed at zero and no payment-received notification went out, with nothing to explain why. The payments are still imported, but the results sheet now says "Success with warnings" and gives the reason on the row, and a warning appears in the import status and your alerts. Verified is also now read correctly whether the cell says Yes, yes or YES — lower case previously counted as No. When two payment rows for the same folio disagree about Verified, the remittance is left unverified and the conflict is reported, rather than the last row silently winning.

### FAQs are one click away from the top of every page
The help icon in the top bar now opens the FAQs page — every question from every user guide on one searchable page. It is available to all staff users, not only to entities with the support chatbot switched on.

## Improvements

### Changing a capital call date keeps the date you typed
When you changed a capital call date and the cascade warning appeared, choosing Cancel put the original date back behind the scenes but left your typed date on screen, so the form showed one date and submitted another. Cancel now restores the field you can see as well. The warning itself also quotes the date in your own format instead of showing it as 2026-02-01.

### Copying a distribution fee to all rows keeps your date format
Copying a fee row down to every other row wrote the date in ISO form (2026-02-01) into a form where every other date read 01/02/2026. Copied dates now appear in your format, and copying an empty row leaves the other rows empty rather than filling them with an invalid date.

### Email previews show KYC document attachments
Previewing an investor KYC email left out documents attached to the KYC record, so the preview under-reported what the email would actually send. Real emails were never affected. The preview now lists the same attachments the recipient will get.

### Identity and bank verification no longer errors on deleted records
PAN and bank verification run a short while after they are requested. If the KYC, offer or interest was deleted in between, the check failed with an error in the background. It now simply stops, since there is nothing left to verify.

## What to Do

| If you use...   | Action                              |
|------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Date filters and date fields | No action needed. You can now type dates instead of using the calendar; check any saved habit of picking dates from grid filters. |
| Remittance payment imports | Re-check recent imports where you set Verified = Yes: if the call was not approved at the time, those remittances are still unverified and need verifying once the call is approved. Future imports will warn you. |
| Capital call date changes | No action needed. |
| Distribution fees | No action needed. Copy-to-all now writes dates in your format. |
| Investor KYC emails | No action needed. Previews now match what is sent. |
