# Release Notes — V9.2.68
**Released: 1 July 2026**

---

## What's New

### Workflows now run on any record type — KYC, commitments, folders, and more
Workflows are no longer limited to a single record type. Your team can now attach and launch workflow templates against investor KYC records, capital commitments, folders, and approvals — all from one consistent workflow engine. KYC workflows can also be configured to start automatically at key moments (onboarding kick-off, form submission, or verification), so nothing slips through.

### Email bounce and complaint tracking for fund managers
When an email you sent through the platform bounces or is marked as spam by a recipient, that event is now captured and visible in a dedicated report. Fund managers and admins can review which addresses are having delivery issues and take action before critical communications are missed. (Not yet live)

### Control email notifications per workflow step
When configuring a workflow template, you can now choose whether the assigned actor receives an email notification when their step becomes active. Toggle it on or off per step — useful when a step is informational only or when the actor prefers to check the workflow dashboard directly.

---

## Improvements

### Deals table now shows pagination and investor names correctly
The deals list view had a layout issue where pagination text was hidden and investor names lacked spacing. Both are now fixed and display clearly.

### E-signature matching now works regardless of email capitalisation
When an investor completes an e-signature via Digio, the email address from Digio is now compared in a case-insensitive way, fixing cases where a capitalisation difference (e.g. "John@firm.com" vs "john@firm.com") caused the signature to not be recognised.

### Logging in from one site now takes you to the right place
If your team uses multiple CapHive sites, logging in via a link from a different site now correctly redirects you to the intended destination rather than landing on the home screen.
