# Release Notes — V9.2.75
**Released: 14 July 2026**

---

## What's New

### Total Income and Compensation figures added to Distribution templates
Fund Term Distribution templates now show a Total Income and Compensation figure (income plus tax plus expenses) for LPs, GPs, individual investors, and KYC groups, across prior, current, and combined periods, with percentage breakdowns included.

---

## Improvements

### Account entry amounts now display and edit correctly
The account entry edit form now pre-fills the amount field instead of leaving it blank, and the entry detail view shows the exact underlying amount on hover and correctly detects percentage-based entries regardless of capitalization.

### Fewer false-positive account lockouts on shared networks
Logged-in users on a shared office or VPN connection are no longer mistakenly caught by the automated protection against repeated bad requests. That protection still applies in full to unauthenticated visitors, and login attempts are still rate-limited as before.

---

## What to Do

| If you use...                          | Action                                                                                     |
|------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Fund Term Distribution reports          | No action needed. Total Income and Compensation figures are now available alongside existing distribution figures. |
| Capital commitment account entries      | No action needed. Editing and viewing amounts now works correctly.                          |
