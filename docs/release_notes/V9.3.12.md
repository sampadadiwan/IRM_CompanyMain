# Release Notes — V9.3.12
**Released: 28 October 2026**

---

## What's New

### Company reports — what the fund bought and sold in the quarter
Each fund in a company report now says whether the fund bought or sold the company during the quarter, and how much, at cost. You can write "The fund invested a further INR 10 Cr during the quarter" and have it appear only when the fund actually invested, instead of looking up the trades and typing them in.

### Consolidated reports — fair market value change follows the value per unit
The fair market value change on each company's fund line, and on the fund total, now shows how much the value of each unit moved, weighted by what each holding was worth at the start. Buying units no longer makes the change look like a large gain, and selling units no longer makes it look like a loss. For example, 10 units at 100 that rise to 110 after 5 more are bought now show +10%, not +65%. The amounts themselves (fair market value now, at the prior period and at financial year start) are unchanged.

### Reports — Gross Portfolio IRR and KPI category for your templates
The consolidated report's fund total now shows the fund's Gross Portfolio IRR (the return on its investments before fund fees and expenses) next to its IRR. The same figure also appears on each company's fund line, and on each fund in a company report. It is the fund's figure, so it repeats for every company in the fund, and shows "N/A" if the ratio was never run. Each KPI row in a company report now also carries its category, so your template can group or label rows by category.

## Improvements

### Percentage KPIs such as EBITDA % now change by percent
A change in a percentage KPI is now shown the same way as every other KPI. A margin that goes from (63.2)% to (55.7)% reads +11.9%, an improvement, instead of a small difference in points that could look like a fall.

---

## Bug Fixes

### Changes from a negative figure now point the right way
When a figure you compare against was negative, a smaller loss showed as a fall and a bigger loss showed as a rise. For example, EBITDA going from (3.15) to (3.80) showed as +20.5% when the loss had grown. A smaller loss now reads as a rise and a bigger loss as a fall. This applies to "vs prior quarter", "vs prior year to date", month-on-month changes, and the difference from Budget in your reports, so the red and green colours on your KPI screens follow too. Changes from a positive figure are unchanged.

### Performance table growth now points the right way
The growth columns on the performance table had the same problem for negative figures, and could show a growing loss in green. They now show the correct direction. No recompute is needed.

## What to Do

| If you use...                                   | Action                                                                                                                                 |
|-------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| KPI changes for companies with negative figures | Open the company's KPI report and run **Recompute percentage change**. Then regenerate any reports you have already produced.          |
| Company report templates                        | To show the quarter's buying and selling, ask your template author to add the new quarter lines. Existing templates work as before.    |
| Reports with Gross Portfolio IRR or KPI category | Ask your template author to add the new fields where you want them. Existing templates work as before.                                |
| Consolidated reports                            | No action needed. Fair market value change will read differently for any period in which units were bought or sold.                    |
| Performance table                               | No action needed.                                                                                                                      |
