# Release Notes — V9.2.89
**Released: 25 August 2026**

---

## What's New

### Portfolio-wide view of board deck and investor update reviews
The Management Accountability agent that flags commitment misses, dropped risks and objective drift in a single company's board decks now has a portfolio-wide dashboard too. See every company's grade at a glance, how scores have moved over time, and a rolled-up view of the same red flags — including a callout for companies that look strong on the numbers but keep dodging accountability in their updates.

### Simpler way to set KPI red/amber/green rules
Setting up automatic RAG status for a KPI no longer means writing a rule by hand. Pick a comparator (Budget, Prior Year, or another figure your portfolio company already reports), whether higher or lower is better, and your red/green thresholds from simple dropdowns and fields — no more getting the format wrong. A new info icon links to a step-by-step guide, and an example gallery shows common setups (revenue vs. budget, margin thresholds, "lower is better" metrics like churn) so you can see what's possible before you configure your own.

### Currency formatting and larger result sets in Report Builder
Reports you build now format monetary columns with the right currency symbol, and can page through much larger result sets than before — both on screen and in your exported spreadsheet.

### Ask Portfolio Assistant questions across your whole portfolio
Portfolio Assistant can now answer questions that span multiple portfolio companies at once, instead of being limited to one company or fund at a time.

---

## Improvements

### AI features are now visible even before you have access
Buttons and menu links for AI-powered features used to disappear completely if your entity wasn't set up to use them. They now always show, greyed out with a tooltip explaining why, so you know the capability exists and who to ask to turn it on.

### Template "Fix Me" catches more problems and shows its work
The AI template repair tool now warns you about pages that look blank because of paragraph styling rather than missing content, fixes indentation issues it previously left behind, and includes a content-check summary confirming nothing was accidentally added or removed — alongside the existing reminder to review AI-generated changes before using them.

### More accurate Portfolio Assistant totals
Fixed an issue where a total in a Portfolio Assistant answer could silently mix figures recorded in different currencies. Answers now also strictly respect what you're authorized to see.

### More reliable rendering for long documents
Fixed an issue where a footnote in a long document could render on the wrong page. Your CapHive team can switch an affected template to a more reliable conversion method if you run into this.
